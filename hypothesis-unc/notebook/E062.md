Predicted accuracy [0.738, 0.756]; got 0.7237. Root cause: I scaled the loss from E006's +0.66 pp (Muon vs SGD at 10 epochs, 432 vs 250 steps). At 226 steps with progressive resizing and freezes, the gap is ~5x larger: Muon's orthogonalized, norm-fixed updates make every one of few steps count, while SGD at the airbench lr needs many more steps. Lesson: Muon's value grows as the step budget shrinks; the optimizer is now a load-bearing part of the recipe.
