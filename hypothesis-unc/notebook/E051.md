# E051 · I080 · H2.5

H2.5 (open, c=0.20): Cutting FLOPs per sample through architecture trades time for accuracy at a better rate than cutting epochs (more than about 1.5 s per pp). E021 suggests small feature maps scale time sublinearly with MACs, so cuts that remove whole high-resolution work (a group-1 conv, backward through early layers) are the remaining candidates.
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus per-layer cost per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and stem backward truncation, made up of a group-1 freeze over the last 25% of steps (−8.8% with no held-out cost, E028, stacked in E034) and a whitening-bias detach after 20% of steps (−2.4%, E036). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017; Fable's CIFAR-10 record agrees). The E043 profile of the 32 px step (33.5 ms): conv fwd/wgrad/dgrad 66%, memory-bound fused BN/GELU/pointwise 27%, NS plus head ~2.5%. The conv kernels already run at high throughput (H2.2.4), so the remaining per-step levers are the memory-bound share (H6.5) and further backward truncation (H2.6, H6.6). Limits: freezing from 60% costs about 0.4–0.56 pp (E037); folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039); a group-2 freeze for the last 10% saved 4.4% but read −0.14 pp (E046, inconclusive).
Idea: Explore/exploit: remove the last full-resolution tensor from the stem. E047 measured g1.conv1 (24→128 at 31×31) at about 0.45× group-2 efficiency, and the 128-channel 31×31 activation feeding the max-pool is the largest memory-bound tensor in the net. E043/E028 showed the stem's 31×31 work costs far more than its FLOP share. On the E036 tip, replace group 1's 'conv1 (3×3, pad 1) → 2×2 max-pool' with a single 3×3 conv with stride 2 and padding 0 (31→15, the same 15×15 output as now, so groups 2/3 and their map sizes 15/7/3 are unchanged at 32 px). At 28/24/20 px it gives 13/11/9, which also matches the current pool outputs; verify this for each resolution in code. Keep the same channel counts, BN-GELU after it, init scheme and RNG draw order (same weight shape). The whitening detach (20%) and group-1 freeze (75%) stay unchanged. Recapture every resolution and variant in build, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on seeds 13–32 against a same-build E036 baseline, plus seeds 0–2. Record the per-step time at 32 px (full and frozen) for the H6 accounting.
Expected (from the queue): conv1 FLOPs fall about 4×, and the 128×31×31 write/read and pool backward disappear. Expect about −3 to −5 ms per unfrozen 32 px step and −1.5 to −2.5 ms per frozen step, proportionally less at low resolution. Total time −5 to −9% (about 4.85–5.05 s). 20-seed mean −0.6 to 0 pp, median −0.25: learned strided downsampling loses max-pool's local translation invariance. It beats the epoch dial (~1.5 s/pp) if the loss is ≤0.25 pp for ≥0.35 s. That would make it the base for an epoch top-up that nets a feasible time below E036. A loss ≥0.4 pp means the stem needs full-resolution conv features and closes the stem-structure branch of H2.5.
