# E039 · I057 · H2.6.5

H2.6.5 (open, c=0.25): During the frozen phase, switching group-1 BN to eval mode (running stats set from a batch-stat pass at the freeze point, then folded into the conv weights) removes the per-step BN statistics passes on the 31×31 maps. Prediction: mean time cut by at least 2% beyond E028's train-mode-BN freeze, with no more than 0.15 pp held-out loss.
H2.6 (supported, c=0.65): Truncating the backward pass at the high-resolution stem late in training cuts per-step cost far more than its FLOP share, at little accuracy cost. The whitening layer and group 1 change little late in training, and their backward on the largest maps is memory-bound. E028 froze group 1 and the whitening bias for the last 25% of steps and saved ~9.5 ms per 32 px step (~27%). Air bench (whitening bias trained for 3 epochs only, 'without reducing accuracy'), Hiverge (whitening bias trained for the first 20% of steps) and FreezeOut (~20% saving with no loss on ResNets/WRNs, including CIFAR-100) are published precedents. Open extensions: freeze earlier, freeze the whitening bias earlier, switch frozen BN to eval mode or fold it into the conv, and stagger the freezes.
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus FLOPs and memory traffic per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004), progressive resizing (−22%, E007; −2.9%, E026) and backward truncation of the high-resolution stem late in training (−8.8% at no held-out cost, E028). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017).
Idea: Explore, the next memory-bound lever on the frozen stem. On the H2.6.2 successor (or the freeze recipe in fcacfc6), at the freeze point: run one no-grad train-mode forward of group 1 over the current batch to set its BN running stats. Use the existing buffers if their values are already the frozen-phase stats. Then, for the frozen phase, put group-1 BN in eval mode and fold scale and shift into the conv weights and biases (done inside timed train, once). The truncated step then runs group 1 as conv+bias+GELU under no_grad, with no BN statistics reductions on the 31×31 maps. Lookahead still averages the unfolded params. Keep the unfolded copies so the final weights and BN buffers stay consistent: either fold only in the compiled frozen forward, or unfold before the final copy. Eval uses the standard model. Capture the new truncated variant in build, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on held-out seeds 3–12 as well as seeds 0–2.
Expected (from the queue): Time falls 1.5–3% (about 0.08–0.15 s) beyond the base. The 3 BN reductions and normalizations on 128×31×31 maps go away from about 55 frozen steps. Held-out within ±0.15 pp: eval-mode BN removes batch-stat noise in the frozen layers, which may even help slightly. A saving under 1% refutes H2.6.5 and says the frozen forward is conv-bound, not BN-bound.
