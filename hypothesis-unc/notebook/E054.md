# E054 · I084 · H2.6

H2.6 (supported, c=0.70): Truncating the backward pass at the high-resolution stem cuts per-step cost far more than its FLOP share, at little accuracy cost if the stem has trained at 32 px. Evidence: freezing group 1 and the whitening bias for the last 25% of steps saved ~23% per frozen 32 px step (E043: 7.7 of 33.5 ms, nearly half of it in memory-bound fused kernels) with no held-out loss (E028), and it stacked with +0.5 epoch (E034). Detaching the whitening output after 20% of steps saved a further 2.4% with no held-out loss (E036). Limits: a freeze from 60% cost 0.4–0.56 pp (E037); a conv1-only freeze from 60% saved 0.9% for −0.21 pp (E042); folding frozen group-1 BN saved nothing (E039); a group-2 freeze over the last 10% saved 4.4% for −0.14 pp against a cross-build baseline (E046, inconclusive).
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus per-layer cost per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and stem backward truncation, made up of a group-1 freeze over the last 25% of steps (−8.8% with no held-out cost, E028, stacked in E034) and a whitening-bias detach after 20% of steps (−2.4%, E036). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017; Fable's CIFAR-10 record agrees). The E043 profile of the 32 px step (33.5 ms): conv fwd/wgrad/dgrad 66%, memory-bound fused BN/GELU/pointwise 27%, NS plus head ~2.5%. The conv kernels already run at high throughput (H2.2.4), so the remaining per-step levers are the memory-bound share (H6.5) and further backward truncation (H2.6, H6.6). Limits: freezing from 60% costs about 0.4–0.56 pp (E037); folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039); a group-2 freeze for the last 10% saved 4.4% but read −0.14 pp (E046, inconclusive).
Idea: Explore, proposing a new H2.6 child: the stem needs a steady adaptation signal, not a gradient on every step. Intermittent stem backward in the 28 px phase, which is the most expensive phase (about 71 steps × ~26 ms ≈ 1.8 s). On the E049 tip, on every second step of the 28 px phase (40–70% of steps), use the group-1-detached 28 px step variant. That variant has no group-1 wgrad/dgrad and no BN/GELU backward on the stem maps, and group-1 params get no Muon/SGD update and no momentum-buffer change on those steps. The variant already existed in E037's commit 6293350. On the other steps, run the usual whitening-detached full step with the group-1 LR as scheduled. Do not rescale the LR: single factor. Group 1 still trains on every step at 20/24 px and from 70% to 75% at 32 px, so it still adapts through the 28→32 switch, which E037/E042 say it needs. Lookahead averages every param. Capture the variant in build with channels_last tensors, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on seeds 13–32 against a same-build E049 baseline, plus seeds 0–2.
Expected (from the queue): About 35 steps save ~5.5–6 ms each, so time falls 0.15–0.22 s (−3 to −4%, to about 4.98–5.05 s). 20-seed mean −0.3 to +0.05 pp (median −0.10). Keep if the loss is ≤0.10 pp and seeds 0–2 ≥0.753 (rate ≥1.5 s/pp, better than the dial). A loss of ≥0.2 pp means stem learning in the 28 px phase is per-step-limited, which closes backward truncation before 75%.
