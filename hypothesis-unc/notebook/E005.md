## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted accuracy [0.747, 0.757]; got 0.7583 (missed high). Root cause: I scaled the expected gain from CIFAR-10 airbench numbers (+0.2-0.5 pp). On CIFAR-100 in a 10-epoch run, the final weights are far from converged and noisy (100-way head, LS 0.3, LR ends at 0.07 of peak), so late weight averaging removes much more variance. The world model underrated how far the 10-epoch endpoint is from a 'settled' solution. Lesson: on CIFAR-100 short runs, end-of-training noise reduction (averaging, lower final LR) is a first-order lever (~1 pp, about 1.5 epochs' worth). Things that cut late-training noise (a lower final LR, a longer decay, BN-stat recalibration) are worth testing next.
