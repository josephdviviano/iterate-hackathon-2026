H2.6.11 (open, c=0.40): The whitening bias finishes learning far earlier than 20% of steps (Hiverge detaches at ~2.6%, airbench96 at 6.7%). On the E049 tip, moving the whitening detach (with its LR annealed to 0) from 20% to 6% of steps cuts mean time by 0.3–0.7% and loses no more than 0.05 pp on fresh seeds 33–52 against E049 on the same seeds. The time effect is below the rebuild time spread (~1%), so judge time on per-step CUDA-event timing, not wall time alone.
H2.6 (supported, c=0.70): Truncating the backward pass saves far more than the truncated FLOP share, but only on layers whose learning has finished. Free cases: the whitening bias after 20% (E036), and group 1 plus whitening over the last 25% (E028, E034: ~23% per frozen 32 px step, nearly half of it memory-bound fused kernels, E043). Group 2 over the last 10% costs about one dial-rate quantity (E046 −0.14 pp for −4.4%; with +0.25 epoch about equal accuracy at −2.1%, E049). Costly cases: stem freeze from 60% (E037 −0.56 pp); conv1-only from 60% (E042 −0.21 pp); intermittent stem backward at 40–70% (E054 −0.48 pp, implementation unverified); group-2 freeze from 85% with an earlier anneal (E053 −0.24 pp, confounded). Folding frozen BN saves nothing (E039).
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, per-epoch cost is a large lever. Levers found: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and backward truncation on layers that have finished learning. Those are the group-1 freeze over the last 25% (−8.8%, E028, stacked in E034), the whitening detach after 20% (−2.4%, E036) and the group-2 freeze over the last 10% (−4.4% at −0.14 pp, E046; with +0.25 epoch about −2.1% at tied accuracy, E049). Systems overhead is exhausted: GPU idle 2.8% plus non-step kernels 2.5% (E047); CUDA graphs and graph breaks were worth <1% (E015, E017, and Fable). Convs run at 200–270 TF/s, except g1.conv1 at ~0.45x, which is only 6–9% of the step (E047). Limits: truncating a layer that is still learning costs at the dial's rate or worse. Examples: stem freeze from 60% −0.56 pp (E037); intermittent stem backward at 28 px −0.48 pp (E054, implementation unverified); group-2 freeze from 85% −0.24 pp (E053, confounded); stride-2 stem −1.1 pp (E051). Folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039).
Idea: Exploit, a near-free truncation. On the E049 tip, move the whitening-bias detach from 20% to 6% of steps (about step 14 of 237, inside the 20 px phase), with the whitening-bias LR annealed to 0 by 6% instead of 20%. The whitening-detached variants already exist at every resolution, so there are no new shapes; error_on_recompile stays on. Everything else is unchanged. Measure the saving with per-step CUDA-event timing (the expected effect is below the ~1% rebuild time spread) as well as wall time. Judge accuracy on seeds 33–52 against same-build E049, plus seeds 0–2. Consult the H7.1.1 curves if they are available first.
