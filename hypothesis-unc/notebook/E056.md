# E056 · I086 · H6.5.1

H6.5.1 (open, c=0.15): On the E036 tip, replacing GELU with ReLU in the conv groups (keeping GELU, if any, before group 1, as in Hiverge, which uses SiLU) cuts mean time by at least 3%. It loses no more than 0.15 pp of the 20-seed mean on seeds 13–32 against a same-build E036 baseline. ReLU's backward needs only a sign mask, so the fused backward reads less.
H6.5 (open, c=0.30): The memory-bound fused BN/GELU/pointwise kernels (27% of a 32 px step, E043) can be shrunk only by using fewer or cheaper elementwise layers, because Inductor cannot fold training-mode BN into a conv epilogue. Activations with cheaper backward passes, or removing a BN/activation from the residual path, cut per-step time roughly in proportion to the activation bytes removed.
H6 (open, c=0.40): Per-MAC cost is not uniform across layers, but the non-uniformity is memory-bound elementwise traffic on large activations, not tensor-core inefficiency. E043: fused BN/GELU/pointwise kernels are 27% of a 32 px step. E047: every conv except g1.conv1 runs at 200–270 TF/s, and g1.conv1 (0.45x) is only 6–9% of the step. Freezing a block also skips its BN/GELU backward and optimizer traffic, which is why the freezes beat their FLOP share (E028, E046: the committee counterexamples). Remaining identical-math levers are small: cheaper activations (H6.5.1, doubted by all members), BN statistics on a subset (new), and Cin padding (H6.4.2, ≤~1.5%).
