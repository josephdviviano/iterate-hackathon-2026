H2.7 (open, c=0.25): Proxy-free loss-based selection: using the main net's own per-sample losses (free from the training forward) to drop the easiest samples from later epochs cuts steps at an accuracy cost no worse than a uniform step trim. Its value depends on whether steps or samples carry accuracy (H5.2). Evidence: E031~ (top-90%: −10.7% time, −0.27 pp held-out, ~2.3 s/pp vs the dial's ~1.5, edge within noise).
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus per-layer cost per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and stem backward truncation, made up of a group-1 freeze over the last 25% of steps (−8.8% with no held-out cost, E028, stacked in E034) and a whitening-bias detach after 20% of steps (−2.4%, E036). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017). Limits: freezing from 60% costs about 0.4–0.56 pp (E037), and folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039).
Idea: Exploit, a milder dose of the E031 near-miss. E031's top-90% selection cost −0.27 pp for −0.63 s (~2.3 s/pp, nominally better than the dial but within noise). On the E036 tip, return per-sample losses from the compiled step (reduction='none', then mean for backward) and scatter them into a GPU buffer indexed by sample id, created and reset in prepare. From epoch 2 on, each epoch's shuffle uses only the 95% of samples with the highest loss in the previous pass (topk on the GPU), with batch 2000 and the ragged batch dropped. Total steps fall from about 231 to about 220. The resolution phases, LR triangle, Lookahead, whitening detach and freeze stay as fractions of the new step count. Warm up the modified step variants in build, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on seeds 13–32 against E036 on the same seeds, plus seeds 0–2.
