H1.1.1 (open, c=0.60): The airbench96-style port trained for 9 epochs with LS 0.3 and no test-time augmentation reaches a 3-seed mean accuracy of at least 0.753, with mean time at most 9.5 s on the A100-SXM4.
H1.1 (open, c=0.75): An airbench96-style net is far more time-efficient than ResNet9 for 75% CIFAR-100. The net has a frozen patch-whitening first conv, Dirac init, 3-conv residual groups with widths about 128/384/576, BatchNorm-GELU, Nesterov SGD (lr 9.0, wd 0.012), batch size 1024, a triangular LR schedule and alternating flip.
H1 (open, c=0.75): The number of epochs needed to reach 75% (sample efficiency) sets most of the wall time. Backbone, optimizer and regularization choices that cut epochs-to-target are the main lever, about 7x over the 40-epoch ResNet9 baseline (59.3 s).
Idea: Replace the template recipe in submissions/arena/ with an airbench96-style port. Architecture: a frozen 2x2 patch-whitening first conv, with whitening computed in prepare from training patches; Dirac init; 3 residual groups of 3 convs at widths 128/384/576; BatchNorm-GELU. Optimizer: Nesterov SGD, lr 9.0 (airbench scaling), wd 0.012, batch size 1024, triangular LR schedule, 9 epochs, label smoothing 0.3. Data: the dataset stays on the GPU in fp16/channels_last; random crop (pad 2) runs on the GPU; derandomized alternating flip. No TTA and no torch.compile yet. Eval is a plain forward pass with no state changes. Build warms up eager kernels and cuDNN on synthetic tensors for train batch 1024 and eval B in {1, 1024}. This is the base recipe every other idea builds on.
Expected (from the queue): First feasible run: accuracy 0.750-0.765, time 8-13 s, complete=True. If accuracy falls below 0.753, raise to 10 epochs before testing anything else (see the H4.2.1 idea).
