H1.3.6 (open, c=0.45): On the E036 tip, recompute BN running statistics for the final averaged weights: one 32 px train-mode forward over ≤5k training images, with BN momentum set so the stats reflect only those batches, inside timed train (<0.1 s). Refuted if the change is ≤0.
H1.3 (supported, c=0.70): In short CIFAR-100 runs, end-of-run averaging is a first-order lever: Lookahead/EMA gave +0.97 pp at E005. The airbench Lookahead schedule is a local optimum on both sides. A harder pull hurt (E025, −0.27 pp held-out), longer free runs hurt (E030, −0.24 pp), and dropping LS late hurt (E032, −0.23 pp), so LS 0.3 regularizes what gets averaged. With averaging on, the final-LR floor does not matter (E009, C0021). The reset probably carries part of the gain (Switch-EMA, C0024). Confirmed in code: airbench's Lookahead lerps and copies back BN running_mean/var along with the weights, so the final BN statistics are a mixture over different fast weights and are never recomputed (search). Untested: BN recomputation (H1.3.6) and plain EMA (H1.3.5).
H1 (supported, c=0.85): The number of epochs needed to reach 75% (sample efficiency) sets most of the wall time. Backbone, optimizer and regularization choices that cut epochs-to-target are the main lever. Together with resizing, compile, averaging and stem backward truncation, they give about 11x over the organizer's 40-epoch ResNet9 baseline (59.30 s on an A100 80GB PCIe, taken from forks of the competition repo and not run in this loop). That is 5.31 s at the E036 tip, which passed the local bar on only 1 of 2 builds (0.7532 in E036, 0.7525 in E038).
Idea: Exploit: a booster that leaves the training trajectory unchanged. It replaces I064 with a paired design that has no trajectory noise. On the E036 tip, at the end of train() and after the final Lookahead copy (which lerps BN running_mean/var from different fast weights and never recomputes them): reset every BN layer's running stats, set momentum=None (cumulative average), run a no-grad train-mode forward at 32 px over K batches of 2000 training images with the usual crop and flip, then restore momentum. Capture this forward-only graph in build on synthetic channels_last tensors, with error_on_recompile on. Eval stays a plain forward pass. Judge it with an offline instrumented copy on unselected seeds 13–32. In each trial, evaluate the averaged model once before recalibration and once after, so the two numbers share the exact same trained weights and the difference has no trajectory or rebuild noise. Do the same for K=1 and K=3. Commit the better K, then run ar.py to get time and the seeds 0–2 check. The submission evaluates only once.
Expected (from the queue): Time rises 0.02–0.05 s (+0.4–1%, to about 5.33–5.36 s). The paired gain per seed should fall between −0.05 and +0.2 pp, median about +0.05. Keep if the mean paired gain is at least +0.05 pp (break-even against the ~1.5 s/pp dial for ≤0.05 s) and positive in at least 13 of 20 seeds. A gain of ≥0.15 pp supports H1.3.6 and pays for about 0.35 epoch of trimming. A gain of ≤0 refutes it: Lookahead's resets every 5 steps already keep the BN stats consistent, which closes the search for a booster that leaves the trajectory unchanged (W3/W6 crux).
