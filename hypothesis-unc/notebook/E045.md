# E045 · I068 · H5.2

H5.2 (open, c=0.30): Early in training (the 20/24 px phases), accuracy is carried by the number of optimizer steps under the LR warm-up and peak, not by the pixels processed. Lowering resolution there at a fixed step count is nearly free (E026, E033), while removing steps costs about as much as a uniform trim (E031, E041, both noise-ambiguous). Corollary: the cheap time levers are those that cut per-step cost at a fixed step count.
H5 (open, c=0.30): At a fixed number of data passes, the optimizer step count (batch size) sets accuracy independently of the epoch count. Muon's critical batch may be smaller than AdamW's (arXiv 2507.01598), and Hiverge's record uses batch 1536 under Muon, which suggests batch 2000 may be above the optimum for a ~220-step run. E006's +0.66 pp is not evidence for this: it confounded Muon, batch 1024→2000 and an RNG re-draw.
Idea: Explore/booster, the step-count crux (W3, W5). E041 found that removing early 20 px steps cost as much as a uniform trim. That suggests early accuracy is carried by optimizer steps, not by pixels. On the E036 tip, use batch 1000 instead of 2000 only during the 20 px phase (the first 30% of data passes, so about 69 steps become about 138). Same data and same pixels; only the step count and per-step overhead change. Key the LR triangle, the whitening-bias detach (20%) and the resolution switch to data progress (samples seen), so they land at the same data point. In this phase, scale the SGD-group lr linearly (×0.5) and keep the Muon lr per step unchanged. Lookahead stays every 5 steps; alpha is near 0 this early. From 24 px on, batch 2000 is unchanged, so the 24/28/32 phases, freeze and Lookahead tail are identical. Capture batch-1000 graphs at 20 px, both the full and whitening-detached variants, in build with channels_last tensors, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on seeds 13–32 against E036 on the same seeds (reuse E038's 13–22 and run E036 once on 23–32; the C/D/selection ideas share this baseline), plus seeds 0–2.
Expected (from the queue): Time rises 0.05–0.12 s (+1–2.5%), from about 69 extra 20 px steps at roughly 1–1.5 ms of overhead each plus lower kernel efficiency at batch 1000. The held-out 20-seed mean should move between 0 and +0.3 pp, median about +0.1. A gain of ≥0.15 pp supports H5.2 and is a better booster than epochs (≥1.5 pp per s). It would also fund E040/E041-style early trims. A change of ≤+0.05 pp refutes the step-count reading: E041's cost then came from compressing the LR schedule, and early batch size is a dead lever (which also settles H5.1.3/H5.2.1 without running them).
