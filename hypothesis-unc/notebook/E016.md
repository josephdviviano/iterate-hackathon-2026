## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted accuracy [0.7505, 0.7605]; got 0.7607 (just above). Root cause: I centred on 'no effect' because I thought head wd mostly sets the logit scale, which LS already caps. But wd also acts on the BN biases at lr_biases = 64 x lr, so the effective decay on the biases is large and pulls every BN shift toward 0 across all 9 conv layers. In a 238-step run that is a real bias. Lesson: the airbench bias_scaler x wd coupling makes bias decay strong; regularizers tuned for 10 classes / longer runs can be too strong here.
