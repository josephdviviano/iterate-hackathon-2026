# E042 · I062 · H2.6

H2.6 (supported, c=0.65): Truncating the backward pass at the high-resolution stem late in training cuts per-step cost far more than its FLOP share, at little accuracy cost. The whitening layer and group 1 change little late in training, and their backward on the largest maps is memory-bound. E028 froze group 1 and the whitening bias for the last 25% of steps and saved ~9.5 ms per 32 px step (~27%). Air bench (whitening bias trained for 3 epochs only, 'without reducing accuracy'), Hiverge (whitening bias trained for the first 20% of steps) and FreezeOut (~20% saving with no loss on ResNets/WRNs, including CIFAR-100) are published precedents. Open extensions: freeze earlier, freeze the whitening bias earlier, switch frozen BN to eval mode or fold it into the conv, and stagger the freezes.
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus FLOPs and memory traffic per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004), progressive resizing (−22%, E007; −2.9%, E026) and backward truncation of the high-resolution stem late in training (−8.8% at no held-out cost, E028). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017).
Idea: Explore/exploit: staggered FreezeOut, which separates where E037's cliff came from. First read group 1's structure in the code. If, as in airbench, the pool follows group 1's first conv, that conv (24→128 on 31×31 maps) and the pool are the only full-resolution work left once the whitening is detached. On the E036 tip, put only group 1's first conv in its own Muon param group. Its LR follows the global triangle to its peak, then is annealed to 0 by 60% of steps. From 60% on, detach that conv's output (before the pool), so there is no conv1 wgrad and no pool/BN backward on the 31×31 maps. The rest of group 1 keeps E036's annealed freeze at 75%. Capture the conv1-detached variants at 28 px and 32 px in build, with error_on_recompile on. Lookahead still averages every param. Judge on held-out seeds 3–12 as well as seeds 0–2. If the code shows the pool is not right after conv1, freeze whichever layers run at 31×31 instead, and record which ones.
Expected (from the queue): About 34 extra steps (60–75%, mostly at 28 px) skip the 31×31 backward, at about 3–5 ms each. Time falls 0.10–0.17 s (2–3%), to about 5.15–5.2 s. Held-out moves between −0.25 and 0 pp. A loss of 0.1 pp or less means E037's −0.56 pp came from freezing the deeper group-1 convs, not the full-resolution conv; that supports staggering, and the next step tests conv1 at 50%. A loss of 0.25 pp or more means the first conv itself must adapt through the 28→32 px switch.
