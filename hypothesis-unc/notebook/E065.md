# E065 · I104 · H2.6.13

H2.6.13 (open, c=0.35): Freeze-dose readings are anneal-plus-freeze composites. On the E061 tip, keep group 1's LR anneal ending at 75% but move only the detach earlier, to 71% (group 1 gets ~9 more steps at a near-zero LR before the detach, then no updates). Prediction: this loses ≤0.1 pp on 3 builds × 20 fresh seeds against E061, far less than E055's −0.29 pp for the same detach point, showing that most of E055's cost came from compressing the group-1 decay over ~23–71% of the run, not from the lost late steps.
H2.6 (supported, c=0.65): Truncating the backward pass saves far more than the truncated FLOP share (H6.6), but only on layers that have finished learning. Free cases: the whitening bias after 6% (E060), and group 1 plus whitening over the last 25% (E028, E034). Group 2 over the last 10% costs about one dial-rate quantity (E046, E049). Costly cases: stem freeze from 60% (E037, −0.56 pp); from 71% (E055, −0.29 pp); conv1-only from 60% (E042, −0.21 pp); intermittent stem backward (E054, −0.48 pp, implementation unverified); group 2 from 85% (E053, −0.24 pp). Caveat: every freeze-dose test moved the anneal end together with the detach point. So E037/E055/E053 are composites of 'lost late steps' and a 'compressed group LR decay over the whole run', and they do not establish a sharp knee at 75% (N5, H2.6.6). The 75% point itself was judged 'free' on 10 seeds (E028, SE ~0.12 pp), so it may cost 0.1–0.2 pp now (N4).
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, per-epoch cost is a large lever. Levers found: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and backward truncation on layers that have finished learning. Those are the group-1 freeze over the last 25% (−8.8%, E028, stacked in E034), the whitening detach after 20% (−2.4%, E036) and the group-2 freeze over the last 10% (−4.4% at −0.14 pp, E046; with +0.25 epoch about −2.1% at tied accuracy, E049). Systems overhead is exhausted: GPU idle 2.8% plus non-step kernels 2.5% (E047); CUDA graphs and graph breaks were worth <1% (E015, E017, and Fable). Convs run at 200–270 TF/s, except g1.conv1 at ~0.45x, which is only 6–9% of the step (E047). Limits: truncating a layer that is still learning costs at the dial's rate or worse. Examples: stem freeze from 60% −0.56 pp (E037); intermittent stem backward at 28 px −0.48 pp (E054, implementation unverified); group-2 freeze from 85% −0.24 pp (E053, confounded); stride-2 stem −1.1 pp (E051). Folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039).
Idea: Exploit: a time-neutral test that separates the two parts of the freeze-dose cost. On the E061 tip (225 steps), change only the end point of the group-1 LR anneal (conv filters on Muon; BN params on SGD): it moves from 75% to 80% of steps, so group 1's LR decays more slowly over the whole run. The group-1 detach stays at 75% (same absolute step), so the frozen variant covers exactly the same steps and time is unchanged. At the detach, the group-1 LR is then about 5/57 ≈ 9% of peak instead of 0. E009 and C0021 say the LR floor does not matter under Lookahead averaging. Whitening detach, group-2 freeze, resolution, step count, Lookahead and LS are unchanged. If the LR is a GPU tensor table, no recapture is needed; otherwise recapture every variant in build, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on 3 fresh builds × seeds 33–52 (60 draws) against E061's 40 draws, then the frozen gate. This is the time-neutral form of H2.6.13. E055's −0.29 pp moved the anneal end and the detach together; this moves only the anneal.
Expected (from the queue): Time unchanged (±0.5%, ~5.02 s). If decay compression drives E055's cost, the 60-draw mean rises +0.05 to +0.15 pp (median +0.07). That is a free booster and would raise the local pass probability from ~50% to ~65%. A change within ±0.03 pp means the cost of earlier freezes comes from lost late steps; the freeze point itself (H2.6.12) is then the lever. A loss ≥0.1 pp means group 1 needs to reach LR 0 before the detach.
