H2.7.1 (open, c=0.20): On the E036 tip, keeping the 95% highest-loss samples from epoch 2 onward (same mechanism as E031; freeze, detach and resolution points kept as step fractions) cuts mean time by at least 4% and loses no more than 0.1 pp of the 20-seed unselected mean vs E036 on the same seeds.
H2.7 (open, c=0.20): Proxy-free loss-based selection: using the main net's own per-sample losses (free from the training forward) to drop the easiest samples from later epochs cuts steps at an accuracy cost no worse than a uniform step trim. Its value depends on whether steps or samples carry accuracy (H5.2). Evidence: E031~ (top-90%: −10.7% time, −0.27 pp held-out, ~2.3 s/pp vs the dial's ~1.5, edge within noise).
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus per-layer cost per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004); progressive resizing (−22% E007, −2.9% E026, −3.2% E033); and stem backward truncation, made up of a group-1 freeze over the last 25% of steps (−8.8% with no held-out cost, E028, stacked in E034) and a whitening-bias detach after 20% of steps (−2.4%, E036). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017; Fable's CIFAR-10 record agrees). The E043 profile of the 32 px step (33.5 ms): conv fwd/wgrad/dgrad 66%, memory-bound fused BN/GELU/pointwise 27%, NS plus head ~2.5%. The conv kernels already run at high throughput (H2.2.4), so the remaining per-step levers are the memory-bound share (H6.5) and further backward truncation (H2.6, H6.6). Limits: freezing from 60% costs about 0.4–0.56 pp (E037); folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039); a group-2 freeze for the last 10% saved 4.4% but read −0.14 pp (E046, inconclusive).
Idea: Exploit, combining two near-misses. Conditional: run only after I075 reports.
