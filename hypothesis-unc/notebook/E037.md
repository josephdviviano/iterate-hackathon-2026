# E037 · I055 · H2.6.4

H2.6.4 (open, c=0.30): Freeze dose: on the N_freeze_epochs recipe, moving the group-1 freeze start from 75% to 60% of steps (group-1 LR annealed to 0 by 60%) cuts mean time by at least 3% more and loses no more than 0.15 pp of the held-out mean. This would beat the epoch dial (~1.5–2.7 s/pp).
H2.6 (supported, c=0.65): Truncating the backward pass at the high-resolution stem late in training cuts per-step cost far more than its FLOP share, at little accuracy cost. The whitening layer and group 1 change little late in training, and their backward on the largest maps is memory-bound. E028 froze group 1 and the whitening bias for the last 25% of steps and saved ~9.5 ms per 32 px step (~27%). Air bench (whitening bias trained for 3 epochs only, 'without reducing accuracy'), Hiverge (whitening bias trained for the first 20% of steps) and FreezeOut (~20% saving with no loss on ResNets/WRNs, including CIFAR-100) are published precedents. Open extensions: freeze earlier, freeze the whitening bias earlier, switch frozen BN to eval mode or fold it into the conv, and stagger the freezes.
H2 (supported, c=0.85): At a fixed epoch count, the cost per epoch (systems overhead plus FLOPs and memory traffic per sample) is a large lever. Levers found so far: compile/fusion (−14%, E004), progressive resizing (−22%, E007; −2.9%, E026) and backward truncation of the high-resolution stem late in training (−8.8% at no held-out cost, E028). Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017).
Idea: Exploit, the freeze dose. Run only once the H2.6.2 successor is kept, and on it. Move the group-1 and whitening-bias freeze start from 75% to 60% of steps. The group-1 LR is annealed to 0 by 60%. Everything else is unchanged, including the step count, resolution phases and LR/Lookahead schedules. The truncated step now also runs during the last 10% of the 28 px phase (60–70%), so capture a truncated 28 px variant in build, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on held-out seeds 3–12 as well as seeds 0–2. Keep if held-out ≥ 0.7545 and seeds 0–2 ≥ 0.753, or if the held-out loss is ≤ 0.1 pp and a follow-up epoch top-up is planned.
Expected (from the queue): Time falls 3–5% (about 0.17–0.25 s, about 33 more truncated steps at about 7–9.5 ms each), to about 5.45–5.55 s. Held-out mean falls 0–0.2 pp. A loss of 0.15 pp or less beats the epoch dial (about 1.5–2.7 s/pp) and supports H2.6.4. A loss above 0.25 pp means group 1 still learns usefully at 60–75% of a 9-epoch CIFAR-100 run, and the freeze stays at 75%.
