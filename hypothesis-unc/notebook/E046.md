Launch overhead is exhausted (E015, E017). Limits: freezing from 60% costs about 0.4–0.56 pp (E037), and folding BN in the frozen phase saves nothing (E039).

On the E036 tip, put the group-2 conv filters (Muon) and group-2 BN params (SGD) in their own param groups. For the last 10% of steps (about 23 steps, 32 px, group 1 already frozen), detach group 2's output before group 3 and use a new compiled 32 px step with no group-1/2 backward and no update for those params. Group-2 BN stays in train mode, and Lookahead still averages every param. Capture the new variant in build with channels_last tensors, with error_on_recompile on. Judge on seeds 13–32 against E036 on the same seeds, plus seeds 0–2.
