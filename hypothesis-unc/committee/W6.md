W6 after E064. Time ≈ 0.1 s fixed + steps × per-step cost. Tip E061: 5.0235 s, 0.7534 on seeds 0–2 (225 steps; population mean ~0.7532).

Latest result: E064 (213 steps) measured 4.838 s and 0.7512 on seeds 0–2. That is -0.19 s for about -0.2 pp on the 3-seed read, roughly 0.9 s/pp, so the early-trim steps are no longer free. The 3-seed noise is ±0.2 pp, so this is weak evidence. E061 had looked free at -0.02 pp.

Mechanisms:
1. Accuracy rises with optimizer steps at about 0.8–1.5 s/pp. Epoch, step and sample cuts all trade near that dial (E020, E031, E041, E045, E048, E061, E064).
2. Early low resolution is nearly free (E026, E033). The 32 px tail carries accuracy (E011, E018, E024).
3. Late backward truncation saves more than its MAC share, because it also skips BN/GELU backward and optimizer traffic. It is free only once a block has finished learning (E028, E036, E046, E049 free; E037, E054, E055 costly).
4. Per-step quality dominates. SGD cost -2.9 pp (E062), ReLU -0.54 (E056), stride-2 stem -1.1 (E051). Structural swaps are mostly negative.
5. Averaging variants, late LS, warm-up, Lookahead interval and BN recalibration are null or negative. wd ×0.5 gave about +0.2 pp. The recipe is near a local optimum for cheap scalar tweaks.
6. Noise: the 3-seed mean moves ±0.2 pp across rebuilds of the same commit. Time moves ±0.05–0.1 s. The 20-seed rebuild SD is ≲0.06 pp. Winner's curse is 0.05–0.15 pp.

My misses: E062 and E056 saved more time than I modelled (the SGD swap saved 0.28 s, ReLU 0.25 s). Both savings came from per-step overhead and kernel cost that I priced too low, so my time model has ±0.1 s of error and I don't treat it as a signal. I also underrated how much per-step update quality matters.

Unsure: whether Muon lr and momentum are mis-tuned, how much of the freeze cost is anneal versus detach, whether the recipe under-fits, and the PCIe offset.

Expectations: tanh-GELU saves little; SiLU saves about as much as GELU. Muon lr and momentum changes are coin flips with a small mean gain. The anneal-only change is near neutral. The Muon-merge systems change saves about 1%. The tip is near its limit: the 3-seed mean sits at about 0.753, so any additional cut will fail the 0.753 gate.
