W3 (cost lens), after E005.

Correction: HYP has epochs=10.0. So E003, E004 and E005 all ran at 10 epochs, not 9. My E005 miss came from pricing it as a 9-epoch run with a small EMA gain.

What was measured:
- E001: eager, 9 epochs, 0.7430, 9.43 s.
- E002: eager, 10 epochs, 0.7495, 10.55 s.
- E003: compiled, 10 epochs, 12.07 s. This was a leak (stride or channels_last guard recompiles inside timed code), not a per-step cost.
- E004: the leak fixed, 10 epochs, 0.7486, 9.04 s. Compile plus fused SGD saves about 14% at unchanged accuracy (−0.1 pp, within noise).
- E005: EMA/Lookahead added, 10 epochs, 0.7583, 9.10 s. That is +0.97 pp for +0.06 s (<1%). By far the best lever per unit of budget.

Budget accounting (compiled):
- Per epoch about 0.85-0.9 s. Fixed cost (prepare, eigh, pad, first steps) about 0.3-0.5 s, under 10%.
- Epoch slope about 0.65 pp per epoch, so 1 pp costs about 1.4 s.
- Current margin: 0.7583 − 0.753 = 0.53 pp. That is worth about 0.8 epoch, about 0.7 s, if the slope holds. Keeping about 0.756 for 40-seed safety leaves only about 0.3 pp, about 0.4 epoch, to cut.
- The cheapest next step is probably epochs 10 → 9.5 (about 8.65 s, about 0.755). Any new lever must beat about 1.4 s per pp.

Progressive resizing (I008):
- FLOPs drop about 25% (0.4×0.56 + 0.3×0.77 + 0.3). On a compiled, conv-bound net, wall time drops perhaps 10-18%: small resolutions underuse the A100, and interpolate, extra guards and kernel selection add cost.
- Risks: compile may recompile or go dynamic on new shapes despite warmup (the E003 lesson). Random-resized-crop also changes the augmentation distribution, and there is a train/test resolution mismatch.
- Expected accuracy cost: 0.4-1.2 pp, which takes about 0.6-1.7 epochs (0.5-1.5 s) to buy back. Net about zero. The hypothesis's ≤0.2 pp bar is likely broken.

Noise: per-seed std assumed 0.2-0.35 pp, 3-seed SE about 0.15-0.2 pp. E003 and E004 had identical accuracy (same seeds, deterministic-ish), so run-to-run accuracy noise at fixed math may be small. Time noise is about 1-2%.

Unsure about:
- whether EMA's +1 pp holds at 9 epochs (it could be larger relative gain, which would allow cutting epochs);
- whether recompiles leak with multiple resolutions;
- whether the slope flattens when EMA is on.
