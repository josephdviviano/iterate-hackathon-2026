W3 (cost lens), after E064.

**Tip (E061, 376f231).** 225 steps at batch 2000. Phases at 20/24/28/32 px run for about 59/24/71/71 steps. The whitening bias is detached at 6%, the stem frozen from 75% and group 2 from 90%. Measured 5.02 s and 0.7534 on seeds 0–2. The population mean is about 0.752–0.7525, so the local bar of 0.753 sits about 0.08–0.1 pp above it. A change that leaves accuracy unchanged passes about 45% of the time; a change that costs accuracy passes rarely.

**Noise.** Per-seed SD is about 0.3 pp, so a 3-seed SE is about 0.17 pp. Within a build, time reproduces to ±0.03 s; across builds it spreads 1–2% (E059: +0.1 s versus the expected value). σ_build is small (E057).

**Time model.** Time ≈ 0.25 s + Σ(steps × per-step cost).
- Per-step cost: 32 px 32 ms, 28 px 26 ms, 24 px 21 ms, 20 px 16 ms.
- A stem-frozen step saves about 8.7 ms; also freezing group 2 saves about 10 ms more.
- E064 removed 12 more 20 px steps and saved 0.185 s, about 15 ms per step, which matches the model. Removals land at or a little under the model.
- Optimizer per-call overhead is about 0.1 s in total (E062: 0.28 s saved against 0.18 s of NS compute). A fused Muon could recover about 0.03–0.06 s.

**Exchange rates.** The dial is about 0.8 s/pp. The second early trim (E064) cost about −0.22 pp at 3 seeds for −0.185 s (about 0.85 s/pp), which is no better than the dial. The free early steps were most likely used up at 225; the 213-step point already sits on the dial line. ReLU (0.46 s/pp) and SGD (catastrophic) are worse. Freezing earlier is steep (E037/E053/E055).

**Mechanisms.**
1. Accuracy is bought with optimizer steps on layers that are still learning (E041/E045/E053/E054/E055/E064 vs E028/E060/E061). Cuts are free only where learning has finished, and those spots are now spent.
2. Muon carries per-step quality: SGD lost 3 pp (E062). Untuned knobs:
   - lr ×1.5: weak positive prior (+0.05–0.1 pp).
   - momentum 0.8: coin flip that leans negative at batch 2000 with Lookahead resets.
3. Lookahead is worth about 1 pp and is already tuned. The LR floor does not matter under averaging (E009), so moving only the anneal end (I104) should be neutral within ±0.1 pp.
4. Filter wd is inert because Muon renormalizes the filters. The BN scale is frozen.
5. GELU's cost is partly erf compute. tanh-GELU could recover perhaps 1–2%, but Inductor computes in fp32 either way and tanh is not much cheaper than erf. I expect the ≥1.5% gate to fail more often than not.
6. Fitting state is unknown. Clean train top-1 is likely about 85–93% with LS 0.3, which would mean mild under-fitting.

**Strategy implication.** No free levers are left on the time axis except systems overhead (I107, about 1%) and possibly activation compute. Accuracy boosters (Muon lr, freeze at 80%) must beat about 0.12 pp per +0.1 s. Most likely the tip stays at 5.02 unless the Muon-lr gain is real and is then spent on a trim.

**Unsure.**

- Inductor's tanh-GELU lowering.
- Size of the fused-optimizer saving.
- PCIe drift.
- Whether E064's −0.22 pp was mostly noise (3-seed SE 0.17).
