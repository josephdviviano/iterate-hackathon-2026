W2 (empirical), after E064. Tip E061: 5.0235 s, 0.7534 (seeds 0-2). E064 (225→213 steps) measured 4.838 s, 0.7512: the extra early trim saved 0.185 s and lost about 0.2 pp on 3 seeds, which is roughly the dial (about 0.9 s/pp) and not free any more.

Mechanisms, each tied to experiments:
1. Epoch dial is about 0.8-1.5 s/pp. E061 was near free (about 0 pp); E064 is at the dial. Step removal costs accuracy beyond about 225 steps (E041, E048, E064).
2. EMA/Lookahead (+0.97 pp, E005) and Muon are load-bearing (E062: SGD costs about 3 pp). More than 10 time-neutral boosters (E025, E029, E030, E032, E044, wd and LS) were flat or negative. Only wd ×0.5 (E016) was a real gain. Expect any further tweak within ±0.15 pp.
3. Time tracks pixels and steps. Early 20 px is nearly free until the step count is cut.
4. Backward truncation was the best lever (E028, E034, E036, E046, E049). Freezing the stem before about 75% loses (E037, E054, E055). Group 2 tolerates a freeze from 85-90%. The whitening detach at 6% saved little (E060).
5. Activation and architecture cuts cost accuracy at the dial's rate or worse (E010, E021, E051, E056).
6. Non-step overhead is about 2.5-5% (E047). A merged optimizer call might save about 1%.
7. Noise: same-commit rebuilds move the 3-seed mean 0.1-0.3 pp and time 0.03-0.05 s. The population mean is about 0.752-0.7535, so headroom is about 0.2 pp. Keep selections carry a winner's curse of 0.1-0.2 pp.

Misses: I over-predicted time when variants cut work (E052, E056, E062). I badly underrated Muon in E062. I under-predicted accuracy on E053.

Unsure: rebuild SD of a 20-seed mean (about 0.06-0.08 pp); whether tanh-GELU or SiLU saves real time; Muon lr and momentum optimum; PCIe judge transfer.
