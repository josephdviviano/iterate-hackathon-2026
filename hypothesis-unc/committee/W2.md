W2 (empirical), after E005.

Measured (3 seeds, all complete):
E001 9 ep eager: 0.7430, 9.43 s. E002 10 ep eager: 0.7495, 10.55 s. E003 compiled (first attempt): 0.7486, 12.07 s. E004 compiled with channels_last warmup fix: 0.7486, 9.04 s. E005 compiled + Lookahead/EMA at 9 ep: 0.7583, 9.10 s (feasible, current best).

Mechanisms:
1. Epoch dial: E001→E002 gives +0.65 pp/epoch and +1.11 s/epoch (about 12%/epoch). Almost all time scales with epochs, so fixed cost is small. This is a regularity over one pair only, and the slope is probably concave.
2. Compile: E003 was slower than eager because of a stride-guard recompile leaking into timed code. E004 fixed it, so the compile cost was a leak, not intrinsic. The compiled 10-epoch run took 9.04 s versus 10.55 s eager. That is about 14% less per run. My earlier claim that compile is net negative was wrong; the lesson is that shape or stride mismatches between warmup and train cost seconds.
3. EMA/Lookahead: E005 against E004 gives +0.97 pp for +0.06 s (about 0.6% time). That is far above the 0.2 pp I expected. Accuracy was at 0.7486 for E004 and 0.7583 for E005. The E004 epoch count is unclear (E003 was described as 9 epochs, but the HYP default is 10). If E005 is 9 epochs and E004 was 10, the EMA gain is even larger. Treat the EMA as worth about 1 pp, about 1.5 epochs.
4. Margin: E005 sits 0.5 pp above the 0.753 bar. Using the 0.65 pp/epoch slope, I could cut about 0.5-0.7 epoch (about 0.6 s) and still clear 0.753. That is a tight, fragile trade.
5. Noise: the 3-seed accuracy mean varies by about 0.15-0.25 pp; time noise is unmeasured but probably 1-2%.

Misses: I under-predicted EMA (predicted 0.745 and got 0.758). I over-predicted the cost of compile once it was fixed.

Unsure: the true per-epoch time after compile; whether the EMA gain holds at fewer epochs; and whether changing resolution triggers recompiles. Each new resolution is a new compiled shape, and the E003 failure shows that unwarmed shapes are costly. Progressive resizing changes the training dynamics, and the BN statistics and the whitening stem see different scales. Reaching 0.753 at 9 epochs with a smaller image for 70% of the run is uncertain.
