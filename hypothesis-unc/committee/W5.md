W5 (contrarian), after E064. Tip: E061, 5.024 s / 0.7534, 225 steps.
1. Noise: per-seed SD is ~0.25 pp and the 3-seed SD ~0.15 pp. Rebuild SD is ~0. Wall time drifts about ±1.5% between sessions, so time differences under ~0.07 s can't be resolved in one run.
2. Mechanism nobody has named: steps at low resolution are host- and launch-bound, not compute-bound. In E061 and E064 each removed 20 px step saved 12–15 ms, against ~6 ms of conv compute at batch 2000. The Python per-step loop does a lot: it rewrites lr for 9 param groups, makes 4 optimizer.step calls (fused SGD plus 3 reduce-overhead Muon cudagraphs, each with an lr_t fill and write-back copies), runs the Lookahead foreach ops and slices the batch. At 20/24 px the GPU sits idle between graphs. At 32 px compute dominates, which is why E047's ~5% idle figure (taken at 32 px) under-counts overhead. Consequences: (a) merging the optimizer calls and moving the LR tables onto the GPU (I107) saves more than the modelled NS netting suggests; (b) a larger batch in the low-res phases (H5.1.3) saves real time; (c) the early trim is a time win but no longer free: E064 read −0.22 pp for −0.19 s, worse than the dial.
3. Muon is load-bearing (E062: −3 pp). Filters are renormalized every step and Muon applies no weight decay, so filter wd is inert by construction (H1.5.4 is settled by the code). Lr and momentum retunes are small effects, ≤0.1 pp, and can't be resolved at 3 seeds. BN scales are already frozen (requires_grad=False), which closes H1.5.10.
4. Accuracy is carried by steps on layers that are still learning. Whitening finishes by ~6%, the stem by ~75%, group 2 around ~90%. Earlier freezes cost (E037, E053, E054, E055). Moving only the anneal end barely matters; moving the stem freeze to 80% trades at about the dial's rate.
5. Bookkeeping caveat: whiten_bias_steps and the freeze steps are computed from planned_steps minus the trim, while the LR peak scales with total_steps. Trims therefore shift the relative schedule positions. E063's crash came from an unwarmed variant or indexing combination at a large trim.
6. Function-changing cuts (ReLU, stride-2 stem, width, SGD) trade worse than the dial. tanh-GELU and SiLU move the same bytes as exact GELU, so their saving is ≤1%. ReLU's 4.8% came from a mask-sized backward plus a cheaper epilogue.
7. The recipe under-fits (LS 0.3, 9.5 epochs). Clean train top-1 is likely 85–92%, so regularizer additions won't pay.
8. The PCIe judge with a slower host will run 5–10% slower, more if it is host-bound.
Unsure about: the size of the per-step host overhead, which needs a profile of a whole 20 px epoch; whether I107 captures it; the cause of E063's crash.
