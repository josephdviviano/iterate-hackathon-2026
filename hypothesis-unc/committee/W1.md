W1 (mechanism), after E030.

**Tip: E027.** E026's math cut to 8.75 true epochs: 219 steps at batch 2000, with 20/24/28/32 px phases starting at 0/15/40/70% of steps.
- Muon on the filters, renormalized every step, so filter wd is inert. Newton-Schulz runs 3 steps.
- SGD wd 0.006 on the BN biases, whitening bias and head. Label smoothing 0.3.
- Lookahead every 5 steps, exponent 3. CUDA graphs.
- Score: 5.878 s, 0.7547. The true mean is probably about 0.753; the held-out mean of the related tip was about 0.7526.

**New results**
- **E030 (Lookahead every 10 steps, same total pull):** 0.7492, −0.55 pp, at the same time. Together with E025 (harder pull, −0.27 pp held-out), this brackets the averaging schedule as a sharp optimum at the airbench values. The Lookahead reset interval matters: resetting fast weights every 5 steps acts as variance reduction for the noisy batch-2000 Muon trajectory. Letting the weights drift 10 steps before the pull leaves the average less well centred.
- **E029 (warm-up halved):** flat to negative, so LR timing is not mis-set.

**Noise**
- Rebuilding the same commit: about ±0.04 pp.
- A trajectory tweak with the same RNG stream: about ±0.2 pp on the 3-seed mean.
- A changed step count or RNG use: about ±0.3 pp.
- Effects under 0.3 pp cannot be read from 3 seeds; the held-out 10 seeds are needed.

**Time model (GPU-bound)**
- My forecasts ran too high for seven rounds, so I now centre lower. Savings are at least proportional to pixel-steps and backward FLOPs.
- Calibration points:
  - 15% of steps moved from 24 to 20 px: −0.18 s.
  - −0.25 epoch: −5%.
  - FreezeOut on group 1 for the last 25%: −13.6%.
- Fixed cost is about 0.2 s. Per-step overhead (Lookahead and optimizer) is a few percent.

**Accuracy exchange rates (pp lost per 10% of time saved)**
- Epochs: about 0.25–0.3.
- FreezeOut: about 0.15, the best rate seen.
- Low resolution before the LR peak: about 0 (E026).
- Low resolution after the peak: worse than epochs (E011, E018, E024).
- Still untested: 20 px across the peak (steps 15–30%). I expect a cost of about 0.1–0.2 pp.

**Mechanisms**
- Lookahead/EMA: +1 pp, and now confirmed near its optimum from both sides.
- Muon plus batch 2000: +0.5 pp, confounded.
- Resizing: −20% time for about −0.5 pp.
- Flat within noise: LS, Muon lr, LR floor, wd ×0.5 and ×0.25, warm-up length.
- Scalar boosters do not exist here. The regime is under-fit and averaging-limited, so every remaining lever is a time cut with an exchange rate.
- Stem whitening mismatch at low resolution is second-order. BN with momentum 0.6 re-estimates scale within about 2 steps, and the whitening bias is trained.
- Loss-based example dropping is mostly a step cut. Under LS 0.3 the easiest examples still give non-zero gradients toward the smoothed target, so dropping them is close to the epoch dial.
  - Implementation risk: if the loss is switched to reduction='mean', the SGD groups (BN biases, head) get 2000× smaller gradients. Muon is scale-invariant and would not care, but the head would stall. That would mean a large accuracy loss.

**Strategy.** The margin is about 0, so only cuts with good exchange rates matter:
1. FreezeOut started later or applied more narrowly.
2. More early 20 px, judged on the held-out mean.
3. Epochs.
