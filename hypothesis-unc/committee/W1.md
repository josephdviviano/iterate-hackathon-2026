W1 (mechanism), after E005.

Correction to my last model: the tip runs 10 epochs (HYP epochs=10.0), not the 9 that the idea texts say. E004 and E005 are both 10-epoch runs.

What has been measured
- 10 epochs, eager: 0.7495 accuracy, 10.55 s (E002).
- 10 epochs, compile + fused SGD: 0.7486 accuracy, 9.04 s (E004). That is 14% faster with the same math.
- E003 lost its time to guard failures and recompiles that leaked out of build, caused by the stride/layout mismatch from a non-channels_last warmup. The stride-guard fix (E004) confirmed this mechanism (a) from my last round.
- 10 epochs, compile + Lookahead/EMA: 0.7583 accuracy, 9.10 s (E005).
  - EMA added about +0.97 pp for about 0.6% extra time. That is far larger than the 0.1-0.4 pp I expected.
  - Mechanism: the alpha ~ t^3 ramp averages the last ~20% of steps. At lr 9 with BN momentum 0.6, the iterates are noisy, and averaging removes the end-of-schedule noise much as a longer anneal would. Averaging the BN buffers keeps the running stats consistent with the averaged weights.
  - At ~0.65 pp/epoch, EMA is worth about 1.5 epochs.

How time arises (compiled)
- About 0.85 s per epoch (48 steps × ~17-18 ms) plus about 0.3-0.5 s fixed cost (H2D copy, whitening eigh, augment).
- Compile depends heavily on warmup matching every shape, stride and dtype used in timed code. Any new shape risks a recompile inside timed code, or automatic dynamic shapes. After a second distinct shape, dynamo in torch 2.4 marks the dimension dynamic, and the dynamic-shape kernels can be slower for every step.
- Per-step cost is only partly FLOP-bound. At 24 px the launch/Python overhead share grows, so savings fall short of the FLOP ratio.

How accuracy arises
- Slope is about 0.6 pp per epoch near 9-10 epochs.
- The tip sits 0.5 pp above the bar. Cutting about 0.6-0.8 epoch (to ~9.2-9.4 epochs) would probably land near 0.754-0.755 and save about 0.6 s. That is the obvious next dial.
- Methods that reduce information per sample cost roughly proportional accuracy:
  - lower resolution, which also adds a train/test mismatch and changes augmentation scale;
  - bigger batches.

Noise
- 3-seed mean std is about 0.15-0.2 pp (E002 vs E004: 0.09 pp apart under the same math).
- Time noise is about 1-2%.
- For the 40-seed judgment, aim for a 3-seed mean of 0.755 or more.

Unsure
- Whether the EMA gain is partly a lucky draw. A +1 pp jump is about 5σ, so the effect is likely real but its size may be +0.6-1.0 pp.
- Whether progressive resizing interacts badly with compile (dynamic shapes) and with EMA (the averaged window falls entirely in the 32 px phase, which helps).
- How much fixed cost remains in prepare.
