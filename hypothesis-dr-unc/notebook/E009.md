## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.749, 0.761]; got 0.7456. Root cause: I transferred the wide net's per-epoch slope (0.87 pp/ep, measured at 8.5-10 ep with cutout + LS 0.2, i.e. strongly regularized and step-limited) to the narrow net with weak regularization. The narrow net at 10-12 ep is closer to its capacity ceiling, so returns per epoch are much flatter. Also the E007 post-mortem lesson ("capacity is not binding") was over-generalized from a single 10-epoch point. What the world model got wrong: the accuracy-vs-epochs slope is not a property of the regime alone; it depends on how close the net is to its capacity/regularization ceiling. Lesson: the per-epoch slope must be measured per architecture; a cheaper per-epoch net is only better if its ceiling is well above 0.753. 128/256/384 ceiling at this recipe is ~75-75.5%.
