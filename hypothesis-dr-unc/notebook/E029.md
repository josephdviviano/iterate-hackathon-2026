## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.748, 0.758]; got 0.7438. Root cause: the resize cost is not linear in the fraction. At 75%, only 57 steps (2.4 epochs) remain at 32 px, and they are all in the low-LR tail of the schedule. The net has too little high-LR time at test resolution to adapt its features (and BN statistics / scale-dependent filters) to 32 px, so test-resolution mismatch dominates. What the world model got wrong: "resize cost ~ FLOPs removed" was fit on 33-50% fractions only; it hid a threshold where the 32 px phase becomes too short. Lesson: keep >= ~half of the steps (or >= ~4-5 epochs) at full resolution; small fraction changes near 0.5 are the safe region; do not extrapolate a linear trade beyond the measured range.
