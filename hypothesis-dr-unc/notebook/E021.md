Within that family, the best time is set by the recipe's accuracy-per-second curve, and that curve cannot be split into epochs x FLOPs/epoch (E009).
Idea: Combine near-misses: put block-3 capacity into the resize recipe. E012 suggests block-3 width is the capacity that 100 classes need. E013 and E017 sit 0.1-0.2 pp short, and extra epochs barely help there (E017 gave +0.11 pp/epoch). Change only block-3 width, from 576 to 704 (3 convs per block, head input widened to match). Block 3 runs at 8x8 (4x4 or 6x6 maps during the 24 px phase), so per-image conv MACs rise only ~0.2 G (~+15%).
Expected (from the queue): Time +0.3 to +0.45 s, to about 6.6-6.8 s. Accuracy +0.2 to +0.5 pp, to about 0.753-0.756. If feasible, this is ~12-14% faster than E012, which supports 'block-3 width is the binding capacity' and the H5 ceiling view: width raises the ceiling where epochs could not. A gain under 0.15 pp means block 3 is not binding either, and the resize line is capped near 0.752 for this family.
