## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.748, 0.756]; got 0.7447. Root cause: with Muon the per-step update size is fixed (orthogonalized, lr 0.24), not proportional to the batch's summed gradient, so 1/3 fewer steps means 1/3 less total Muon movement; the "sample-limited" finding (E015, E024, E028) was about SGD's per-example lr and held only while steps were plentiful (>= 210). What the world model got wrong: it treated the batch axis as optimizer-independent. Lesson: under Muon, batch increases must come with a Muon lr increase (or more epochs); the step count (~200+) is itself a resource. Muon lr scaling with batch is the follow-up if the batch axis is reopened.
