## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.751, 0.759]; got 0.7504. Root cause (hypothesis): my minimax fit targeted singular values down to 0.03 of the top one and pushed them all to ~1. For a minibatch gradient, the small singular directions are mostly noise; inflating them to unit size adds noise at full step size. airbench's quintic (and the 5-step Polar Express set's early steps) leaves the smallest directions damped, i.e. it implicitly filters noise. The airbench constants were tuned empirically on CIFAR, which favours a slightly "sloppy" orthogonalization. What the world model got wrong: "more exact orthogonalization = better Muon" (the literature's claim is about LLM training at long horizons). Lesson: in this regime Muon's NS acts partly as a spectral filter; don't sharpen it. If revisited, try l0 larger (e.g. 0.1) or fewer iterations rather than tighter.
