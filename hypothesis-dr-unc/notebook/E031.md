# E031 · I044 · H1.1

H1.1 (open, c=0.35): The accuracy-vs-epochs slope is not a fixed property of the regime. It depends on architecture and recipe and flattens near the recipe's ceiling: 0.87 pp/ep (E002, wide, heavy reg) versus 0.27-0.52 pp/ep on later recipes. It must be measured per architecture and recipe over spans of >= 2 epochs or with replicated endpoints. Interpolating a slope from another recipe is unreliable (E009).
H1 (open, c=0.75): Sample efficiency dominates time-to-75%. An airbench-style whitened CNN (frozen patch-whitening, dirac init, BN, Nesterov SGD, label smoothing, Lookahead) reaches 75% on CIFAR-100 in ~10-11 epochs, and with resize, block-specific widths and fused updates it gets there in 6.65 s (E023), against 59 s for the organizers' ResNet9. Within that family the best time is set by trading levers at their marginal rates: resize costs ~0.4 pp per second saved, block-3 width buys ~0.6 pp/s, and epochs buy ~0.5-0.75 pp/s near 10-11 ep. Single-run rates carry about +/-0.2-0.3 pp/s of error, so these rates rank levers only loosely.
