Predicted time [6.70, 6.82]; got 6.69. Root cause: my estimate came from profiling the E012 net (11 ep all at 32 px, 14 ms steps); the E021 recipe has half its steps at 24 px (~9 ms), where fixed per-step overheads like the optimizer are a larger share and the CPU may even be close to the critical path. What the model got wrong: per-step overhead is constant in absolute terms, so its relative cost grows as steps get cheaper (resize, narrower nets). Lesson: as steps get cheaper, the remaining per-step host overhead (lr loop, lookahead, gathers) becomes worth attacking; re-profile on the current tip before estimating.
