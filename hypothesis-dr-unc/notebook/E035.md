## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.748, 0.757] (point -0.2 pp); got 0.7470 (-0.81 pp). Root cause: I underrated weight averaging in a short, noisy, high-lr run. With only 210 steps and the LR ending at 7% of peak (not 0), the final iterate is noisy; Lookahead's slow weights average the last tens of steps and act as the annealing that the schedule does not fully provide. Muon's fixed-size orthogonalized steps do not shrink with the gradient near convergence, so the iterate noise is if anything larger than under SGD. What the world model got wrong: it treated Lookahead as an inherited SGD-era detail. Lesson: averaging is first-order here; test stronger/longer averaging (e.g. higher alpha, more frequent sync, or an EMA tail) as a margin source rather than removing it.
