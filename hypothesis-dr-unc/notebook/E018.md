# E018 · I022 · H3.2.3

H3.2.3 (open, c=0.45): A 24/28/32 px curriculum by thirds of steps (bilinear downsample of the full augmented image, all shapes compiled and graphed in build) on the E004 recipe at 10.5-11 epochs reaches 3-seed accuracy >= 0.753 at a mean time <= 8.2 s. It loses less accuracy per second saved than the 50%-at-24 px schedule.
H3.2 (open, c=0.60): Progressive resizing: training early steps at reduced resolution cuts their cost (a 24 px step costs ~0.68x a 32 px step here, E008). The accuracy cost (~0.55 pp for 50% of steps at 24 px) takes fewer extra epochs to repay than the resizing saves, so resizing plus extra epochs reaches 0.753 faster than E004.
H3 (open, c=0.45): Doing less compute per epoch beats running fewer epochs: hard-example filtering and progressive resizing cut FLOPs per useful gradient enough to lower time-to-75% on CIFAR-100, even in a short, under-fitting schedule.

Downsample the augmented batch with bilinear F.interpolate (antialias=True), with the LR schedule tied to step count. Compile and CUDA-graph all three shapes on synthetic data in build, and check that no recompile lands in timed train. Compare it with E013's 50%-at-24 px (0.41 pp lost per second saved).
Expected (from the queue): Time about 6.4-6.6 s (the 28 px third costs ~0.85x). Accuracy about 0.752-0.755 (a loss of 0.15-0.45 pp vs E012). Supports H3.2.3 if the loss per second saved is < 0.3 pp/s, vs ~0.4 pp/s for the 50%-at-24 schedule; refutes it if the loss is >= 0.4 pp/s.
