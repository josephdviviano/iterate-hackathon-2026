## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,

Predicted accuracy [0.718, 0.740]; got 0.7402 (just above). Root cause: I assumed capacity matters a lot for 100 classes in an under-fitting regime (-1.5 to -3 pp). Actually 128/256/384 lost only 1.5 pp despite 40% fewer FLOPs. What the model got wrong: at <= 10 epochs the bottleneck is optimization progress (steps), not capacity; the wide net's extra capacity is mostly unused. Lesson: in this regime, shrink width before cutting epochs; expect diminishing accuracy loss from width reduction until the net becomes really small.
