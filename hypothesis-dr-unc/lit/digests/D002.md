## Lit digest: recovering ~2.3 pp at an ~8-epoch CIFAR-100 budget

**Bottom line:** none of the cards give accuracy-vs-epoch or accuracy-vs-width numbers for fast CIFAR-100 training without TTA. Every card is from a ResNet, EfficientNet or MobileNet run at 25–100+ epochs. Our own runs already answer the question better than the papers do:

- **Epochs:** going from 8.5 to 10 epochs (E001 → E002) gave about **+0.87 pp per epoch** for about 1.0 s per epoch.
- **Less regularization:** at 8.5 epochs, E004 (no cutout, label smoothing 0.1) gave **+2.45 pp**. That is about 3× what more epochs buy, and it costs no extra epochs.

So retuning regularization (H4.1) is the cheapest lever, and it is already feasible: 0.7550 at 8.94 s.

**Cards:**
- **C0013** (1cycle on ResNet-56 for CIFAR-100: 25 epochs beats any step-schedule result). *Conditional.* It says the schedule shape matters a lot under short budgets. Our linear warmup/decay is already close to 1cycle, so the gain left over is probably small. A peak-LR sweep would be a cheap check.
- **C0011 / C0012** (the UBA schedule wins at 25–50% of the full budget). *Conditional.* Tested on ResNets with much longer runs. Low priority: try it only after the regularization and epoch levers are used up.
- **C0014** (wide nets converge in 8× fewer epochs with 1cycle; CIFAR-10, 100 epochs). *Conditional.* It weakly favors more width, not the narrowing in H1.2. It isn't strong enough to reorder the plan.
- **C0015 / C0018** (under a budget, importance sampling doesn't beat uniform sampling; variety matters more). *Contradicts* H3.1 (proxy-loss filtering). Lower H3.1/H3.1.1 confidence. If it is tested at all, use one decisive run.
- **C0016 / C0017** (more augmentation preserves accuracy at a 30% budget cut). *Conditional.* That is a mild budget cut. In our heavily under-fitting 8-epoch regime, E004 showed the opposite: removing cutout helped. Treat these cards as not applying below about 15 epochs.
- **C0019–C0022, C0023** (EfficientNet/MobileNet trained for 100 epochs; AdamW vs SGD on CIFAR-10). Not relevant to this regime. Ignore them. H4.2 stays low priority.

**Not in the tree and not covered by any card:** EMA or weight averaging, and how batch size interacts with LR at this budget. Both are worth a Lvl2 node under H4.

**Next steps:**
1. **Check the timing.** E004 took 8.94 s but E001 took 7.77 s at the same 8.5 epochs. Removing cutout should not *add* time. Check whether max-autotune or graph capture leaked in from E003, or whether this is run-to-run noise. The repeat-run noise floor from H1.1.1 is still not reported.
2. **Push regularization further:** label smoothing 0.0–0.05 and translate 2. Bank the accuracy gain.
3. **Trade the margin for epochs:** at about 0.9 pp per epoch, each 0.9 pp above 0.753 buys back about 1 epoch (about 1 s). E004's margin is only 0.2 pp, so this step depends on step 2 working.
4. **Width scaling:** hold it until 2 and 3 are done.
