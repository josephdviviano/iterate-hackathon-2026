**Digest: spending Muon's margin on resize (I039 / E029)**

**What was asked, and what was found.** None of the retrieved cards covers Muon's learning rate or momentum. On those two settings there is no literature evidence here. The cards that are relevant cover progressive resizing, which is the lever I039 actually changes.

**What already happened.** E029 has already run this idea. It got 0.7438 at 5.09 s, which misses the 0.753 bar. Compared with the Muon base, the cost per second saved is much steeper than the old ~0.4 pp/s line:
- **Against E025** (0.7622, 7.26 s): about 0.85 pp/s.
- **Against fused E026** (0.7628, 6.97 s): about 1.0 pp/s.

Cutting epochs under Muon was cheaper. E027 went from 11 to 9.5 epochs and lost 0.37 pp for 1.18 s saved, about 0.31 pp/s. So the premise that "resize is the cheapest lever, epochs cost 0.75 pp/s" no longer holds on a Muon base. That 0.75 pp/s rate came from E020, which was run with SGD.

**What the literature says**
- **C0086** (EfficientNetV2, ImageNet): progressive resizing speeds up training but costs accuracy. **Explains** E008, E013, E018 and E029. Action: keep it as background for H3.2. It does not say where the cost becomes nonlinear.
- **C0087 / C0089**: lowering regularization during the small-image phase, and raising it again as resolution grows, recovers the accuracy lost to resizing. They report this on ImageNet and also on CIFAR, Cars and Flowers. **Conditional**, because it has not been tested here. This is the mechanism behind H3.2.4: scale translate down to ~1-2 px during the 24 px phase. Action: run H3.2.4 before raising the 24 px fraction any further. A 75% low-res phase with full-strength augmentation is the case where it should matter most.
- **C0079** (FixRes, ImageNet): the training resolution limits accuracy at a fixed test resolution (2.7 pp between 128 and 224 px). **Untested** mechanism. In E029 only the last 25% of steps run at the 32 px test resolution, while learning rate and Lookahead are already annealing. The frozen whitening filters, which were fitted at 32 px, and the BN statistics may not have time to re-adapt. If that is the cause, the cost should depend on how long the final 32 px phase is, and should jump sharply rather than grow linearly with the low-res fraction. The tree has no node for this.
  - Action: add a Lvl3 under H3.2. At a fixed total number of 24 px steps, compare "24 px phase, then ≥40% at 32 px" against the E029 split. Or test 62.5% at 24 px to locate where the cost turns steep.
- **C0084**: the best resolution choice depends on the dataset. **Conditional**, and weak. Treat rates from CIFAR-10 or Fulcrum as priors only.

**Bearing on the tree**
- **H3.2.5 is refuted.** Its claim that the cost "does not steepen at higher fractions" failed in E029. Lower H3.2's confidence for fractions above 50%.
- **H1 needs its marginal rates updated** for the Muon regime: epochs at ~0.3 pp/s, resize above 50% at ≥0.85 pp/s.
- **Next spend.** Put Muon's margin into epoch cuts (from E027/E028) or batch size, not into more resize.
- **Muon lr and momentum remain an open gap.** A small sweep around 0.24 / 0.6 at 9.5 epochs is cheap and has no prior from these cards.

## Claims
- C0086 (explains; H3.2, H3.2.5): Progressive resize trades accuracy for speed. This explains the losses in E008, E013, E018 and E029. Keep it as background; it does not fix where the cost turns nonlinear.
- C0087 (conditional; H3.2.4, H3.2, H4.1): Weaker regularization during the low-res phase recovers the resize loss (ImageNet/CIFAR, untested here). Run H3.2.4 (translate ~1-2 px during the 24 px phase) before raising the 24 px fraction again.
- C0089 (conditional; H3.2, H3.2.4): Progressive resize plus adaptive regularization beats a static resolution. Supports pairing any larger low-res fraction with scaled-down augmentation.
- C0079 (untested; H3.2, H3.2.5): Train/test resolution discrepancy: a final 32 px phase that is too short (25% in E029, during annealing) may not re-adapt BN and features to the whitening fitted at 32 px. Add a Lvl3 that varies the length of the final 32 px phase at fixed low-res steps to test for a sharp jump in cost.
- C0084 (conditional; H3.2, H1): The best resolution depends on the dataset. Treat resize rates from CIFAR-10 or Fulcrum only as priors and measure them on CIFAR-100 with Muon.
