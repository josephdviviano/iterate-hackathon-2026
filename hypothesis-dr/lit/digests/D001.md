This supports the H1 premise that schedule-driven sample efficiency dominates. But the regime is 25 epochs on ResNet-56, not about 10 epochs on an airbench-style net, so it says nothing about where the 0.753 knee falls. The tree also has no node for schedule shape (warmup fraction, peak lr, decay to zero). That is a gap worth one Lvl3 prediction once a base recipe exists.
