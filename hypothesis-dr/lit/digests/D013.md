## Batch-size scaling for Muon in ~200-step training: what the cards say

**The short answer: none of the cards answer (a)–(d) for Muon in a ~200-step regime.** The empirical cards come from SGD, momentum SGD and Adam (Smith et al. 2017). The momentum cards are kernel-regression theory, and the one Muon card is an asymptotic bound. None gives a critical batch size for Muon, and none covers the averaging horizon. Treat everything below as a prior, not a rule.

**What the loop has measured.** On top of E040 (0.7559, 5.72 s), going to bs 1536 (E041) cost ~0.26 pp and saved 5.2% of time. That is ~0.05 pp per 1% of time. Cutting Muon epochs costs ~0.08–0.15 pp per 1%. So bigger batches have been the cheapest time lever so far, and bs 2048 is worth one run. The open question is whether the cost per 1% climbs past ~0.08 at 2048, which would mean we have crossed the critical batch size.

**Cards that bear on the decision**
- **C0179 (linear lr scaling, SGD, B/N≪1): explains a choice already made.** This is the rule behind the x1.5 SGD-group lr in E041. At 2048, bs/N is still ~4%, so x2 relative to bs 1024 (keeping lr·wd constant) should still hold for the BN biases and the head. It says nothing about Muon's lr. Normalized updates make "no scaling" the default there, and that default is still untested.
- **C0178 / C0183 / C0181 / C0182 (raising the batch can replace lr decay, at equal noise scale ηB/N; held for SGD, Nesterov and Adam over long runs): conditional.** This is the argument for a batch ramp (b): 1024–1536 early, 2048 late, with roughly 2.7x fewer late steps. Muon's update size is fixed, so batch size only changes noise in the update direction. The equivalence probably does not carry over to the Muon group. It may carry over to the SGD group. One caveat: our lr already decays to a 0.07x floor. A late large batch on top of that cuts noise twice, and E025 showed that removing tail noise hurts (decay-to-0 cost 0.51 pp). If we ramp, consider pairing it with a higher floor. New hypothesis for H1.2.3.
- **C0180 (raising SGD momentum from 0.9 to 0.98 while scaling batch cost ~1 pp): conditional, and it answers (c).** Do not raise momentum when steps are cut. If momentum is tied to samples, 0.8 at bs 1024 becomes ~0.64 at bs 2048. Muon momentum was flat between 0.6 and 0.8 (E026), so 0.7 is a safe middle value.
- **C0174 / C0175 / C0176 (momentum enlarges the critical batch size; Nesterov does better at large batch): untested here.** This is weak theoretical support for keeping Nesterov momentum on both groups at 2048.
- **C0177 (Muon's critical batch size bound; Nesterov does not change the asymptotic rate): untested.** It gives no usable number.

**Averaging horizon (also part of c).** Nothing in the literature covers it. If we keep the sync at every 5 steps, bs 2048 leaves only ~32 syncs. Scale the sync interval by samples instead (every ~3 steps at 2048).

**What to do.** Run one bs-2048 test at 6.5 epochs: Muon lr 0.16, momentum 0.7, SGD-group lr x2 relative to bs 1024 with lr·wd constant, lookahead sync every 3 steps, jitter kept. If accuracy falls more than 0.2 pp below E042, test the late-phase ramp (1536 → 2048) instead.

## Claims
- C0179 (explains; H1.2.3): Explains the x1.5 SGD-group lr choice in E041; at bs 2048 scale the SGD group x2 vs bs 1024 with lr*wd constant. It says nothing about the Muon lr; keep it at 0.16.
- C0178 (conditional; H1.2.3, H1.6): Basis for a batch ramp (small batch early, 2048 late). It assumes SGD-style noise scale, which may not hold for Muon's normalized updates. Pair any ramp with a higher lr floor to avoid the E025 tail-noise loss.
- C0183 (conditional; H1.2.3): Raising the batch late cut SGD updates ~2.7x with no accuracy loss. Use it as the prior for a 1536->2048 late-phase ramp if fixed bs 2048 costs >0.2 pp.
- C0181 (conditional; H1.2.3): A large-batch accuracy gap appears only when the noise path is mismanaged. Keep the SGD group's effective noise scale matched when raising the batch.
- C0182 (conditional; H1.2.3): The equivalence held for Nesterov and Adam, but Muon was not tested. Do not assume it transfers to the conv-filter group.
- C0180 (conditional; H1.2.3, H1.2): Raising momentum while scaling the batch cost ~1 pp (SGD). At bs 2048 do not raise Muon momentum; use ~0.7, or 0.64 if momentum is tied to samples.
- C0174 (untested; H1.2.3): Theory: momentum enlarges the critical batch size. Weak support for keeping momentum on at bs 2048.
- C0175 (untested; H1.2.3): Theory: Nesterov is best in the large-batch regime. Keep Nesterov on both groups.
- C0176 (untested; H1.2.3): Nesterov lowers the noise floor at high momentum and large batch. Supports keeping Nesterov; it does not settle the momentum value.
- C0177 (untested; H1.2.3): Asymptotic Muon critical-batch bound with no usable number. Must be measured empirically (bs 2048 run).
