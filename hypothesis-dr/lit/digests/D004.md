### What the loop's own results imply

- **Correction to the question:** the current best is E010, not E007: **0.7551 accuracy at 8.09 s**. The amax-pool change (supports H2.2) took about 0.17 s off E007. Systems work is not fully exhausted, but each change gains only about 2%.
- **Noise floor:** E003 reproduced E002's accuracy exactly (0.75523). On the 3 fixed seeds, accuracy is effectively deterministic, so even a 0.1 pp difference in a single run is real *for these seeds*. The 40-seed official judgment still adds sampling risk that 3 seeds cannot measure. A margin of about 0.2 pp, as at E010, is thin.
- **Optimizer dynamics matter:** removing lookahead cost 1.1 pp (E008, supports H4.2.1). That is about 2 epochs' worth, given that 8.5 → 9 epochs moved accuracy by about 0.4 pp (E006 vs E002). An optimizer change has room to beat schedule tuning, but this is a prior, not evidence about Muon.

### Recommendation

With no literature, H1.2.1 has to be settled by direct experiment, and the test should be cheap and unconfounded:

1. **Port Muon for 4D conv filters only.** Flatten each filter to (out, in·k·k). Keep Nesterov SGD for BatchNorm biases, the whitening layer (frozen), and the head. **Keep lookahead and the 9-epoch schedule unchanged**, and leave batch size at 1024. Moving to bs 2000 at the same time would make any result impossible to attribute. Use the airbench94_muon-style defaults in H1.2.1 (lr ~0.24, momentum 0.6, Nesterov, 5 NS steps) as an unverified starting point. Compare against E010 at matched epochs: accuracy is the primary readout, and time shows the NS overhead.
2. **If accuracy rises ≥0.4 pp at 9 epochs**, test 8 epochs next; that is where a time win would show up. **If it falls**, try one lr ×2 / ×0.5 pair and drop lookahead only as a separate arm. Lookahead's averaging may overlap with Muon's update normalization, but that is untested here and in the literature found.
3. **If Muon fails**, stay with SGD. The best-supported lever left is averaging (EMA on top of lookahead, H4.2), because E008 shows that is where the large effects are.
