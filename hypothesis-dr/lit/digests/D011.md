# D011: On the Muon + lookahead tip (bs 1024, 6.5 epochs, ~49 steps/epoch, CIFAR-100), can the batch size be raised to 1536-2048 without losing accuracy per epoch? Fewer steps would cut the per-step Muon and lookahead overhead and give larger GEMMs (H1.2.3, untested). The search should find what the literature says about the critical batch size of orthogonalized and normalized-update optimizers (Muon, Shampoo/SOAP-like) compared with SGD. It should also cover how lr, momentum, weight decay and averaging horizon should be rescaled when the batch size grows at a fixed epoch budget in short, data-limited training. That evidence decides whether to run the batch-size experiment next and how to set the SGD-group lr/wd and the lookahead schedule for it.

## Batch size on the Muon tip: what the literature says

**Bottom line:** No card tests Muon at large batch on a small, data-limited vision task. The Muon evidence comes from LM pretraining or theory. It favours trying bs 1536, not 2048, with momentum and the SGD group adjusted as below.

**For larger batches**
- **C0138 (conditional):** Muon keeps its data efficiency well past AdamW's critical batch size (CBS). This was measured in LMs up to 4B parameters with huge token budgets.
- **C0137 / C0136 (conditional):** the optimal batch size depends on the optimizer, and momentum or adaptive methods tolerate larger batches than SGD.
- **C0129 (conditional):** Muon's gradient variance scales with effective rank, not with full dimension. This is a mechanism for a higher CBS.
- Together these justify running H1.2.3.

**Against**
- **C0131 / C0134 (contradicts):** CBS grows with data size, not model size. We train on 50k images for about 6.5 epochs, roughly 320 steps. That is the regime where CBS is small, so dropping to about 213 steps (1536) or about 160 (2048) may cost accuracy per epoch.
- **C0127 (explains/conditional):** steps-to-target falls with B, but with diminishing returns.
- **C0142 (conditional):** large batches lose generalization unless the noise scale is preserved.

**How to rescale**
- **C0141 (conditional, SGD-family):** at a fixed epoch budget, accuracy holds if the noise scale lr·N/B is kept.
  - Scale the SGD group's lr (BN biases, whitening bias, head) by B/1024.
  - Leave wd unchanged. lr·wd per step then rises by the same factor, so decay per epoch stays the same.
  - Keep the Muon lr unchanged, as H1.2.3 says. Note that fixed-size updates over 1.5× fewer steps mean less total movement.
- **C0128 (untested here):** the theory says CBS falls as momentum β and wd λ rise. **C0143** found that raising momentum along with batch size cost 0.9 pp on CIFAR-10. Both point to using Muon momentum 0.6 rather than the tip's 0.8 at the larger batch. E026 shows the two are flat at bs 1024, so this costs nothing to try.
- **C0144 (untested mechanism):** BN noise shrinks as the batch grows (ghost BN fixes the ghost-batch size). This is a hidden loss of regularization. If bs 1536 loses accuracy, ghost BN at 1024 is the follow-up.
- **C0132 (explains):** weight averaging helps most at large batch. This fits E020/E008, where averaging was worth about 1 pp. Keep lookahead with sync every 5 steps. Keep the base decay at 0.97^5: E028 shows the horizon is flat, so the fewer iterates should not need retuning.

**Decision**
- Run H1.2.3 at **bs 1536**, 6.5 epochs, with these settings:
  - SGD-group lr ×1.5, wd unchanged
  - Muon lr 0.16, momentum 0.6
  - lookahead unchanged
  - new full and partial batch shapes warmed up in build()
- **Break-even test:** the Muon epoch slope is about 1.14 pp per epoch for about 15% of time, so 1% of time is worth about 0.075 pp.
  - A 5% time cut pays for itself only if accuracy drops by less than about 0.37 pp.
  - The tip (E034, 0.7551) has only about 0.2 pp of headroom over the gate, so a drop above 0.2 pp also needs extra epochs.
  - Pre-register: a drop of 0.25 pp or more with time down less than 5% refutes H1.2.3 for this regime. In that case skip 2048.
- C0130, C0133, C0139, C0140 and C0145 are not relevant here. Conv-filter wd is moot under the weight-norm projection.

## Claims
- C0138 (conditional; H1.2.3): Muon retains data efficiency at large batch only shown in LM pretraining; justifies testing bs 1536 but not a prior for data-limited CIFAR-100
- C0137 (conditional; H1.2.3): Momentum/adaptive optimizers have larger optimal batch than SGD; supports trying larger batch on Muon base
- C0136 (conditional; H1.2.3): Optimal batch is optimizer-dependent; SGD-era bs 1024 need not be optimal for Muon
- C0129 (conditional; H1.2.3): Low-rank variance bound gives mechanism for higher Muon CBS; theory/FashionMNIST only
- C0131 (contradicts; H1.2.3): CBS scales with data size; 50k x 6.5 epochs (~320 steps) is a small-CBS regime, so expect accuracy loss per epoch at 1536-2048; prefer 1536 first
- C0134 (contradicts; H1.2.3): SGD theory: CBS grows with data, lr and B must be rescaled jointly; reinforces caution in data-limited short training
- C0127 (conditional; H1.2.3): Steps-to-target decreases convexly in B with diminishing returns; gains from 1536->2048 likely smaller than 1024->1536
- C0142 (conditional; H1.2.3): Large-batch generalization gap unless noise scale maintained; motivates SGD-group lr scaling
- C0141 (conditional; H1.2.3, H1.7): Preserve noise scale at fixed epochs: scale SGD-group lr by B/1024, keep wd so lr*wd per epoch unchanged; Muon lr kept
- C0128 (untested; H1.2.3, H1.2): CBS decreases with momentum and wd; run bs 1536 with Muon momentum 0.6 instead of tip's 0.8 (flat at bs1024 per E026)
- C0143 (conditional; H1.2.3): Raising momentum with batch size cost 0.9 pp on CIFAR-10; do not raise Muon momentum when growing batch
- C0144 (untested; H1.2.3): BN noise shrinks with batch; ghost BN at 1024 is the follow-up if bs 1536 loses accuracy
- C0132 (explains; H4.2, H1.6): Weight averaging aids large-batch efficiency, consistent with E008/E020 ~1pp; keep lookahead with sync 5, base 0.97^5 (horizon flat per E028)
