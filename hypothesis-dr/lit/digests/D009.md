# D009: On the Muon tip (6.5 epochs, bs 1024, lookahead, CIFAR-100, ~320 steps total), can the batch size be raised (1536-2048) at a fixed epoch count without losing accuracy? This would cut per-step Muon and lookahead overhead and enlarge the GEMMs (H1.2.3). If so, how should the hyperparameters scale with batch size: the Muon lr and momentum, the SGD-group (BN biases, head) lr and wd, the warmup length, and the averaging horizon? This is the largest untested time lever. The last three tail and schedule tweaks (E025, E031, E033) all failed. Evidence on critical batch size for orthogonalized or normalized optimizers in very short, few-hundred-step training would decide whether bs 1536 is worth a run. It would also set the scaling rules for that run.

## Batch size on the Muon tip (H1.2.3): what the literature says

**Short answer:** No card measures critical batch size (CBS) for a normalized or orthogonalized optimizer in a CNN run of a few hundred steps. The evidence comes from LLM pretraining or toy theory and points both ways. No card gives scaling rules for Muon lr or momentum, the SGD-group lr and wd, warmup, or the lookahead horizon. bs 1536 is worth one pre-registered run, but only as a test of whether we are below CBS.

**For larger batches (all conditional, regimes not tested here):**
- Muon saturates at a larger CBS than SGD (C0106, linear associative memory) and keeps data efficiency well past AdamW's CBS (C0103, C0104; LLMs up to 4B parameters).
- Momentum-type optimizers can use larger optimal batch sizes than plain SGD (C0101).
- CBS ∝ gradient variance (C0097). A 100-way head with LS 0.2 may have higher per-sample σ² than CIFAR-10, which would help 1536. That fits Hiverge and airbench94_muon running bs 1536-2000 at ~8 epochs on CIFAR-10.

**Against (conditional, but the most relevant to our regime):**
- C0110: the optimal batch size grows with training length, roughly ∝ T^0.5. Our ~320-step budget is tiny, so our CBS is probably small.
- C0098/C0099: above the optimal batch size, extra batch does not cut the steps needed. At fixed epochs, bs 1536 removes a third of the steps (~319 → ~212). Below CBS, that costs accuracy directly.
- Two of our own results say the recipe is step-starved and fragile. E019 lost 1.04 pp when Muon took larger steps, and E022 → E027 cost ~0.15 pp per quarter epoch. The tip E034 sits at 0.7551, only ~0.2 pp above the gate. Even a 0.3 pp loss would have to be bought back with ~0.5 epoch, which is about 7% of time.

**Size of the time lever.** Muon plus lookahead overhead is ≲10% of the step (H2.4). A third fewer steps saves about 3%, plus whatever larger GEMMs add. The run must keep accuracy within ~0.15 pp to come out net positive.

**Scaling rules for the run. None of these come from the cards; label them as heuristics:**
- Keep the Muon lr. Normalized updates fit this, but C0097 and C0100 say nothing about lr transfer.
- Keep Muon momentum at 0.8.
- Scale the SGD-group lr ×1.5 and keep lr·wd constant.
- Keep the warmup *fraction*.
- Sync lookahead every 3-4 steps so the averaging horizon stays the same measured in samples.

**Other notes:**
- C0111 (untested mechanism): in µP, CBS did not drift with model width. So width changes such as E029 should not change the batch-size choice.
- C0105 (untested): µP and Muon hyperparameter transfer applies to widths, not to this question.

**Action:** run bs 1536 at 6.5 epochs on E034 and treat it as a CBS probe. If mean accuracy falls by >0.3 pp on all three seeds, CBS is below 1536 at this horizon: lower H1.2.3 and leave bs 2048 untried. If it holds within ~0.15 pp, follow up with bs 2048.

## Claims
- C0106 (conditional; H1.2.3, H1.2): Muon tolerates a larger CBS than SGD, which argues bs 1536 may be safe. The evidence is toy linear-memory theory only, so it should not raise confidence much.
- C0103 (conditional; H1.2.3): Muon keeps data efficiency well past CBS in LLM pretraining. Supports running a bs 1536 probe; transfer to a ~300-step CNN run is untested.
- C0104 (conditional; H1.2.3): Muon stays compute-efficient at large batches (LLM regime). Consistent with the per-step overhead argument in H1.2.3.
- C0101 (conditional; H1.2.3): Momentum-type optimizers support a larger optimal batch than SGD. Keep momentum at 0.8 or higher when raising bs.
- C0097 (conditional; H1.2.3): CBS scales with gradient variance. A noisier 100-way task may tolerate a larger bs. Treat this as weak support only.
- C0110 (conditional; H1.2.3): The optimal batch size grows with the training horizon, so a ~320-step budget implies a small CBS. This is the main risk for H1.2.3; pre-register a >0.3 pp drop as a refutation.
- C0098 (conditional; H1.2.3): There is an optimal batch size beyond which steps stop shrinking. At fixed epochs, bs 1536 cuts steps by a third and loses accuracy if we are above it.
- C0099 (conditional; H1.2.3): Above the optimal bs, steps do not decrease. Ties into the step-starvation seen in E019/E022-E027; read a bs-1536 accuracy loss as CBS < 1536.
- C0111 (untested; H1.2.3, H1.5.2): CBS did not drift with µP width. Width reallocation (E029, H1.5.2) should not change the batch-size decision; keep bs and width as separate levers.
