## Rescaling Muon and its SGD group for larger batches (bs 1024 → 1536/2048)

**Bottom line:** the cards support a square-root lr rule for Muon and a linear rule for the SGD group. None of them gives Muon's critical batch size on CIFAR, and none covers batch-scaled momentum.

**Muon lr against batch size.**
- **C0208 (explains E046):** when gradient noise dominates, normalized SGD behaves like plain SGD with a rescaled lr. Seesaw's matching rule for normalized or Adam-like updates is lr ∝ √B.
  - For 1024 → 1536 that gives 0.16 × √1.5 ≈ 0.196. That is almost exactly E046's 0.20, which measured +0.25 pp and recovered most of E041's −0.26 pp loss.
  - The loop discarded E046 as noise, but the literature predicted both the direction and the rough size.
  - Action: treat 0.20 as the principled bs-1536 setting rather than a tuned keep. Either re-grade it on extra seeds (H4.1.2/H4.1.4 tooling) or bundle it into the next bs run.
  - Caveat: Muon orthogonalizes rather than norm-normalizes, so the mapping is approximate.
- **C0201 / C0202 (conditional):** for sign-like optimizers the optimal lr rises with batch size up to a point B*, then falls back toward a non-zero floor. If Muon follows this, √B scaling fails past B*. Running out of lr returns at bs 2048 would be a sign that B* has been passed. This is shown for Adam, not Muon.

**SGD group (head, BN biases, whitening bias).**
- **C0203 / C0205 (explain the x1.5 SGD rule and fit E045):** for SGD, what matters is keeping lr/B fixed (halving the lr matches doubling the batch). Linear lr scaling of the SGD group is the right default, so use x2 at bs 2048. E045 (head lr x2, kept) adds that the head was already under-driven before any batch change.

**Critical batch size.**
- **C0207 (conditional, for H1.2.3/H5):** past the critical batch size, accuracy per sample drops whatever lr–batch scaling is used.
- E041 lost 0.26 pp and a √-scaled lr appears to win most of it back. That points to bs 1536 being at or just below the critical batch size here, not beyond it.
- No verified card gives a Muon-vs-SGD critical batch size for CIFAR. The Muon critical-batch-size paper was captured only for its Nesterov result.
- Gate bs 2048 (~160 steps) on whether bs 1536 with lr 0.20 holds ≥0.753. If it does, try bs 2048 with Muon lr ≈ 0.226 (0.16 × √2) and SGD lr x2.

**Warmup and momentum.**
- **C0204 (untested):** large batches with high initial momentum can be unstable early, and a reduced early lr is the fix. Keep warmup as a fraction of samples, i.e. do not shorten it in samples.
- No card covers scaling the momentum horizon with batch size, so a momentum retune at bs 1536 has no literature support. It is lower priority than the lr fix.
- **C0200 (untested, minor):** Nesterov changes Muon's convergence bounds only slightly. Not a lever.

**Ghost BN, NS iteration count, lookahead interval:** nothing found in the cards.

**Suggested order:**
1. bs 1536 with Muon lr 0.20, confirmed on extra seeds.
2. bs 2048 with lr scaled by √B for Muon and linearly for the SGD group.
3. Ghost BN only if step 2 falls more than 0.25 pp short.

## Claims
- C0208 (explains; H1.2.3): √B lr rule for normalized updates predicts Muon lr 0.16→~0.196 at bs 1536 = E046; treat 0.20 as the principled default and re-grade on extra seeds
- C0201 (conditional; H1.2.3, H1.2): Optimal lr for sign-like optimizers may fall past B*; if a √-scaled lr fails at bs 2048, take that as having passed B*
- C0202 (conditional; H1.2.3): The lr optimum approaches a non-zero floor at very large B; don't cut Muon lr to rescue bs 2048
- C0203 (explains; H1.2.3, H1.7.3): Keeping lr/B fixed justifies SGD-group x1.5 at bs 1536 and x2 at bs 2048
- C0205 (explains; H1.2.3): Same lr-batch equivalence for SGD; scale the companion group's lr linearly
- C0207 (conditional; H1.2.3, H5): Past the critical batch size, no lr scaling recovers accuracy per sample; gate bs 2048 on bs 1536 with lr 0.20 holding ≥0.753
- C0204 (untested; H1.2.3): At large batch keep warmup a fixed fraction of samples; don't shorten it as steps drop
- C0200 (untested; H1.2): Nesterov matters little for Muon; not a lever for the batch move
