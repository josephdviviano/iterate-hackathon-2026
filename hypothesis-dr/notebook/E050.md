## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.747, 0.759] (point -0.2 pp); got -1.41 pp, with almost no time saved (-0.8% vs -2% expected).
Root cause: a qualitative change at 16px that I extrapolated linearly from 24 -> 20 (-0.18 pp). At 16px block 3
runs at 3x3 and pools to a 1x1 final map, so the global max-pool becomes an identity over one position. The head and
block 3 then learn a different feature geometry (no spatial max selection) in the very phase that sets up BN and
whitening-bias statistics; the 32px stages afterwards have to re-learn it. On time, small maps are
launch/latency-bound, so the FLOP savings did not materialise. The world model assumed smooth degradation with
resolution. Lesson: check structural thresholds (final map size, pooling semantics) before extrapolating a
resolution trend; keep every curriculum stage's final map >= 2x2.
