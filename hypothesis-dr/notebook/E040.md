# E040 · I046 · H2.3.2

H2.3.2 (open, c=0.25): On the Muon base, a bilinear whole-image resolution curriculum (first third of epochs at 24 px, middle third at 28 px, last third at 32 px) with total epochs raised ~15% reaches >=0.753 at >=5% less time than the tip. It needs per-resolution compiled graphs and a flatten(2).max(2) global pool, checked with debug_nan.
H2.3 (open, c=0.30): Progressive resizing lowers total FLOPs with little loss in final accuracy if it is done as a curriculum of bilinearly downsampled whole images (e.g. 24 -> 28 -> 32 px) with some epochs added, rather than as low-res random crops at a fixed epoch count. Each resolution is compiled in build(), and the global pool must not assume a 4x4 final map.
H2 (open, c=0.50): With the recipe and epochs held fixed, per-step GPU work can still be cut by a few percent without changing accuracy. The work to remove is in non-conv kernels (pool backward, elementwise activations), in the many-small-kernel Muon optimizer step (still ~1.43 ms/step for ~0.2 ms of NS FLOPs), in avoidable backward work (whitening-bias gradient), and in conv-shape inefficiencies. Algorithm selection is not the source. Systems keeps so far total ~8% (E007, E010, E021, E023), and ~5-8% may remain.
Idea: Reallocate time from full-res epochs to cheap low-res epochs. The Muon epoch slope at the knee is now steep (~1.14 pp/epoch, ~0.15 pp per 1% time). E013 priced 24 px epochs at only ~0.058 pp per 1% on SGD, so low-res epochs should now be cheaper than full-res ones. On the tip (E034 commit f74c715: Muon NS3 batched, lr 0.16, mom 0.8, lookahead 0.97^5, LS 0.2, SiLU, whitening bias trained only in epoch 1, ~6.5 epochs), make one curriculum change. Train the first 1.5 epochs on whole images bilinearly downsampled to 24 px (F.interpolate, antialias=True, inside the compiled GPU augment, after flip/translate). Then train 5.25 epochs at 32 px, for 6.75 epochs total. Scale the lr, Muon and lookahead schedules to the new total step count; bs stays 1024. The frozen whitening conv is reused unchanged. In drafts/, first print the per-block spatial sizes at 24 px input. Set the final pool for each resolution graph to F.max_pool2d with a static kernel equal to that graph's final map size. Do NOT use amax or flatten().max (the E009 Inductor bug). Compile and warm up every variant in build() on synthetic data: 24 px with whitening-bias grad, 24 px without it, 32 px without it, plus each full and partial batch shape. Eval stays the 32 px graph. Run drafts/debug_nan.py on every graph under max-autotune-no-cudagraphs before launching.
