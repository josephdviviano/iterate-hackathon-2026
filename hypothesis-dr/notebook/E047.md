H2.3 (open, c=0.40): Progressive resizing lowers total FLOPs with little loss in final accuracy if it is done as a curriculum of bilinearly downsampled whole images (e.g. 24 -> 28 -> 32 px) with some epochs added, rather than as low-res random crops at a fixed epoch count. Each resolution is compiled in build(), and the global pool must not assume a 4x4 final map.
H2 (open, c=0.50): With the recipe and epochs held fixed, per-step GPU work can still be cut by a few percent at unchanged accuracy. The cuts must remove GPU work, not launches: E005, E038 and Fable's CUDA-graph result show launch overhead is hidden at bs >=1024. Keeps so far total ~11%: E007 ~1% (possibly jitter), E010 2.0%, E021 2.2%, E023 2.7%, E034 3.0%. Remaining candidates: Muon weight-norm projection frequency, NS iteration count or preconditioning, training the whitening bias for <1 epoch, and the resolution curriculum (H2.3, a FLOP cut that trades some accuracy). Conv-shape and algorithm-selection levers are <=~1% (E007, E032).
