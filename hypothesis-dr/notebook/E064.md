H2.4.7 (open, c=0.10): FreezeOut-style: freeze block 1 (conv weights and BN biases) for the last ~20% of steps through a compiled graph variant that detaches block 1's output. This skips block 1's wgrad and its Muon work, cutting mean time by >=3% with accuracy within -0.2 pp. Prior is low: there was no gain on VGG-like nets, and E037 showed block-1 capacity matters under Muon.
H2.4 (supported, c=0.80): Non-conv work that takes a visible share of GPU time can be cut with no change to accuracy by doing less of it. Four instances so far: the atomic adaptive-pool backward (E010, -2.0%), GELU -> SiLU (E021, -2.2%), a batched compiled Muon step (E023, -2.7%), and freezing the whitening bias after epoch 1 so block 1's first-conv dgrad is skipped (E034, -3.0%). The remaining Muon cost (~1.4 ms/step) is GPU kernel time, not launch gaps (E038). Candidates left: weight-norm projection frequency (H2.4.3), fewer or preconditioned NS iterations, and training the whitening bias for 0.2 epoch (~0.5%, below jitter).
H2 (open, c=0.55): With the recipe held fixed, per-step GPU work can still be cut by a few percent at unchanged accuracy. The cut must remove GPU work, not launches; launch overhead is hidden (E005, E038). Systems keeps total ~11% (E007 ~1%, E010 2.0%, E021 2.2%, E023 2.7%, E034 3.0%). The resolution curriculum (H2.3) has added FLOP cuts: E040 -3.0%, E047 -3.5%, E048 -5.2%. Candidates left: Muon projection frequency (H2.4.3), NS2 with per-iteration (Polar Express) coefficients, re-checking max-autotune (H2.5.1), and trial-0 warmup gaps (H3.2). Conv-shape levers are <=~1% (E032).
Idea: Partial freezing by update thinning, as an explore against a hard earlier freeze. On the tip (E058 or its successor), make one change: during the 32 px stage before the existing freeze (samples 175000-270000, epochs 3.5-5.4), run the existing block-1-frozen graph pair (detached block-1 output, and the Muon variant without the 128x1152 group) on every odd step, and the normal graph pair on even steps. On frozen steps block 1 gets no gradient: its Muon momentum buffer is not updated and its BN biases are skipped in SGD, while its BN running stats still update. The lr schedules are unchanged, so block 1 takes half as many rotations in that window, each from a full-batch gradient. Pick the variant in Python by step parity, with no data-dependent branch. No new graphs are needed, since both variants already exist at 32 px. Warm up any new partial shape at the 3.5-epoch and 5.4-epoch switches with both variants in the exact timed layout (E035). Compare trial-0 with later-trial times.
