H2.4 (supported, c=0.80): Non-conv work that takes a visible share of GPU time can be cut with no change to accuracy by doing less of it. The remaining Muon cost (~1.4 ms/step) is GPU kernel time, not launch gaps (E038). Candidates left: weight-norm projection frequency (H2.4.3), fewer or preconditioned NS iterations, and training the whitening bias for 0.2 epoch (~0.5%, below jitter).
