## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [4.44, 4.56] (point 4.50); got 4.567. The prepare saving (-0.047 s) materialised exactly; the train-side saving
(-0.03 s, compiled lookahead + whole-epoch resize) did not, and train time rose 0.016-0.054 s per trial with an upward drift
across trials (the E075 host-contention pattern). Root cause: sub-1% train-time gains measured offline with synchronized
timers sit inside harness-level run-to-run train jitter (~+-1%). The world model treated offline-measured saves as additive
and deterministic in the harness. Lesson: only prepare-side or >~1.5% train-side savings are visible in one harness run.
