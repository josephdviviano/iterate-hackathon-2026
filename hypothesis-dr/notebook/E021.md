## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Accuracy predicted [0.752, 0.762] (point -0.1 pp); got 0.76203, just above the top. I centred on a small loss from
leaving GELU, but the outcome was a +0.24 pp re-draw. The root cause is a range too narrow for per-seed re-draw: the
3-seed mean re-draw sd is ~0.1-0.15 pp, so a point estimate +- 0.5 pp barely covers +-2.5 sd once a small expected
shift is added. Lesson: for "neutral" changes, centre on the tip and use at least +-0.5 pp plus the expected shift.
