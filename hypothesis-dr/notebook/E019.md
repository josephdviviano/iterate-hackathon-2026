## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted accuracy [0.754, 0.764] (point +0.05 pp); got 0.7492 (-1.04 pp). I assumed a flat lr optimum near 0.24 (the
airbench94_muon value). Root cause: with weight-norm projection every step, the Muon lr is the per-step relative
rotation of each filter (an orthogonalised update of spectral norm ~1 against rows of norm ~1). That is a direct,
unbuffered noise scale at the end of training. The schedule floor of 0.07x keeps 0.36 x 0.07 = 0.025 relative steps in
the final iterations, which lookahead cannot fully average out. The world model had no view of how Muon's lr interacts
with the lr floor. Lesson: Muon lr acts like SGD's lr/weight-norm ratio and is sensitive; test lower (0.16) and a
lower end-of-schedule floor for the Muon group before assuming any optimum.
