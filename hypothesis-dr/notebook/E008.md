# E008 · I006 · H4.2.1

H4.2.1 (open, c=0.45): Removing lookahead from the airbench96-style base costs >=0.3 pp of mean accuracy at a fixed epoch count, equivalent to about 1 epoch, and raises the per-seed accuracy std.
H4.2 (open, c=0.50): Weight averaging (lookahead or EMA) raises accuracy at low epoch counts and lowers the spread across seeds. This allows fewer epochs at the same safety margin.
H4 (open, c=0.50): The variance across seeds and runs, measured against the 0.753 cutoff on 3 seeds, decides how much over-training is needed. Measuring the noise floor and reducing variance (lookahead/EMA) lets a recipe run closer to its knee, below 0.758 without being fragile.
Idea: Base recipe at its knee epoch count with lookahead removed: plain Nesterov SGD, no weight averaging. Same lr, wd and schedule.
Expected (from the queue): Accuracy drops ~0.3-0.6 pp (worth about 1 epoch) and per-seed spread widens. Time falls slightly (<2%) from skipping the averaging step. Confirms lookahead is worth keeping.

accuracy 0.7443 (seeds 0.7464, 0.7413, 0.7453) vs tip E007 0.7542 (0.7536, 0.7521, 0.7568): -0.99 pp, all three seeds
down 0.7-1.2 pp. time 8.335 s vs 8.257 s (+0.9%, the wrong direction). Infeasible -> discard.
Verdict supports H4.2.1 (drop >= 0.3 pp, ~3x the size I expected). Contingency "drop > 0.6 pp" fired: lookahead is a
major lever here (worth >1 epoch: at ~0.3-1 pp/epoch near the knee, roughly 1-2 epochs). Its schedule (decay
0.95^5 x (t/T)^3, every 5 steps) is untuned for CIFAR-100 and could be worth tuning.
Time: removing lookahead should save ~0.06 s, yet time rose 0.08 s. Train times per seed 8.20-8.22 vs 8.12-8.16 in E007.
The E008 build reused cached Inductor autotune results (4 autotune lines vs 10 in E007), so kernel choice should match.
Most likely explanation: run-to-run timing jitter of ~+-1% (E003 also drifted +0.9% on an identical commit).

## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Accuracy predicted [0.745, 0.756], got 0.7443: the drop was ~3x my point estimate (-0.25 pp). I assumed lookahead's value
shrinks with horizon, as on CIFAR-10. In this 9-epoch CIFAR-100 regime it is worth ~1 pp. The world model
underrated weight averaging; averaging is one of the strongest levers in the recipe.
Time predicted [8.08, 8.30], got 8.335: I treated timing noise as ~0 because E003 was 0.9% off and I took that as "small".
But 1% is as large as the effects I am now trying to measure (E007 kept at -1.1%). Lesson: time differences < ~1.5%
from a single run are not distinguishable from jitter; E007's -1.1% keep is itself within noise.
