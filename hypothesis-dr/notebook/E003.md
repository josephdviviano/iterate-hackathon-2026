Diagnostic profiling done after this run (drafts/profile_*.py, not experiments):
- Tight loop of train_step on distinct real batches: 18.8 ms/step, flat over 8x48 steps (no thermal drift);
  432 steps x 18.8 ms = 8.12 s of the 8.3 s train time.
- Torch profiler, per step: conv backward ~8.5 ms, conv forward ~4.1 ms (cuDNN cutlass/xmma bf16 kernels, ~75%
  of GPU time), Triton fused elementwise (BN/GELU/pool) ~3.4 ms, adaptive_max_pool2d backward 0.48 ms (atomic kernel),
  SGD step 0.44 ms. GPU time ~ wall time: NOT launch-bound.
- Per-epoch overheads: augment 8.9 ms, randperm+gather 2.2 ms; lookahead update 0.7 ms every 5 steps. Total ~0.16 s/run (~2%).
Implication: I009 (fully compiled step + fused optimizer) can save at most the 0.44 ms SGD step plus some launch
overhead, ~2-3%; H2.2.1's ">=8%" premise (launch-bound) is contradicted by the profile.
