H2.2 (open, c=0.50): Hiverge-style engineering cuts the remaining per-step overhead in a run of a few seconds: a vectorized or fused optimizer update (foreach/fused, or batched Muon), a fully compiled train step, and SiLU instead of GELU.
H2 (open, c=0.60): With the recipe and epochs held fixed, per-step GPU efficiency is a major share of what remains of the time. That covers precision, memory format, kernel fusion and optimizer or Python launch overhead. Systems work can cut time by 10-30% with no change in accuracy.
Idea: One systems change on the tip recipe (E007 commit 52204ad: 9 epochs, LS 0.2, cudnn.benchmark plus max-autotune-no-cudagraphs). Replace the final global adaptive_max_pool2d (and any other adaptive pool in the net) with an equivalent reduction, x.amax(dim=(2,3)), or F.max_pool2d with a fixed kernel equal to the spatial size. Its backward then becomes a dense masked/eq kernel that Inductor can fuse, instead of the atomic scatter kernel that took 0.48 ms/step (~2.5% of GPU time) in the E003 profile. The forward math is identical. Recompile and warm up in build() for the full batch, the partial batch and eval shapes. Nothing else changes.
Expected (from the queue): Time -1% to -2.5% (8.05-8.18 s vs 8.257 s). Mean accuracy within ±0.15 pp of 0.7542; only tie-breaking in the max gradient can differ. If time improves by less than 0.8% (inside timing jitter), drop pooling as a lever. If the change helps, re-profile and take the next-largest non-conv kernel.

## Outcome
<!-- After the run: the numbers, which contingency fired, the verdict and why. -->
Crash: "Classifier returned nonfinite logits" on trial 1. Diagnosis (drafts/debug_nan.py): parameters go NaN after the
first step. Eager backward of x.amax(dim=(2,3)) is finite, but the Inductor-compiled backward (max-autotune-no-cudagraphs,
bf16, channels_last) gives NaN grads in whiten.bias and group-0 weights: a torch 2.4 Inductor bug in amax backward,
not a problem with the idea. Contingency "crash": dumb and fixable -> use F.max_pool2d(x, 4) (the alternative named in
I014), which trains finitely for all 432 steps; relaunch I014 with that. Verdict "-".

## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted a finite, ~1.3% faster run; got a NaN crash. Root cause: compiled amax backward is broken in this pinned
torch 2.4 Inductor (eager is fine). The world model treated "same math" as "same behaviour under compile". Lesson: after
any change to the compiled graph, run a one-step finite-gradient check (drafts/debug_nan.py) before launching.
