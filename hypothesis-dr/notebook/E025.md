## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted +0.1 pp (supports >= +0.2 pp); got -0.51 pp. Root cause: the E019 post-mortem story ("late Muon noise that
lookahead cannot average out") was wrong in direction. E019's loss came from a higher lr over the WHOLE run, not from
the tail. With lookahead heavily weighting late iterates, the tail lr acts as exploration that the average exploits;
removing it leaves the average of near-identical iterates. The world model over-generalised from one lr result to the
tail. Lesson: separate "lr level" from "tail shape" hypotheses; with lookahead/EMA, decay-to-zero is not a safe default.
