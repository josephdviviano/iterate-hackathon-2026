## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Accuracy predicted [0.744, 0.765] (point +0.3 pp); got 0.7681 (+1.3 pp). The world model treated the optimizer as
a modest lever (D002/D003 had no evidence; I discounted the airbench94_muon CIFAR-10 gain and expected the
bs-1024 / warmup-schedule mismatch to eat into it). In fact, in this short, 100-class regime, the update geometry
(orthogonalised, normalised conv-filter updates) is the biggest single lever found, larger than lookahead (~1 pp).
Lesson: in a very short horizon, per-step update quality dominates; optimizer changes deserve wider ranges and
higher priority than architecture or schedule tweaks.
