or extra low-res epochs added on top of 9 full-res epochs (a cheap 0.53 s epoch instead of a 0.9 s one).

## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,

Time predicted [7.05, 7.70], got 6.97 (better than predicted). I assumed small spatial maps would run well below
FLOP-proportional efficiency, but 24px epochs cost 59% of a 32px epoch for 56% of the FLOPs. The world model
over-weighted GPU under-utilisation at small sizes: at bs 1024 even 3x3 maps keep the A100 busy. Lesson: time scales
close to FLOPs for this net down to 24px at bs 1024, so FLOP counts are a good time predictor.
