## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted -0.08 pp (from E063's +0.07 pp for +0.25 epoch); got -0.63 pp for -0.25 epoch. I read E063 as a flat local
epoch slope and assumed symmetry. Two things broke that: (1) E063 was measured on a different recipe (block-1 freeze at
0.7, which is itself infeasible), so its "+0.07" is one noisy paired point (paired 3-seed sd ~0.14 pp); (2) the accuracy
curve near the knee is steep and convex: the tip sits where removing steps hurts far more than adding them helps
(E006 showed the same at 8.5 vs 9 epochs with SGD). Lesson: never infer a slope from a single +delta run on another base;
the epoch knee is asymmetric, so price cuts with a direct cut experiment, not by mirroring an addition.
