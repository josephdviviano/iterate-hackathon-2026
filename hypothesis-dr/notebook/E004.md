## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Hypothesis refuted (predictions hit). The tree assumed 100 classes call for stronger LS than CIFAR-10's 0.2; the data
says LS between 0.2 and 0.3 is flat at 9 epochs. Lesson: the E003 "noise floor" (bit-identical reruns) measures only
nondeterminism, not seed sensitivity. Any recipe change re-draws each seed's outcome with per-seed sd ~0.2 pp,
so a 3-seed mean difference needs to exceed ~0.2-0.25 pp before it says anything about the 40-seed mean.
