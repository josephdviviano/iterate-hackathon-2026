This also tests the E004 power-cap lesson, which predicts little gain from cutting overhead alone.

### Post-mortem
Predicted time [7.12, 7.24] (hit) and accuracy [0.751, 0.758] (hit). Failure only in feasibility: a numerically
different but equivalent optimizer moved seed 2 by -0.75 pp (0.7561 -> 0.7486), consistent with
per-seed perturbation noise of ~0.4 pp. Lesson: the E001 tip's 0.19 pp margin cannot absorb *any* perturbation
reliably; to keep small engineering wins we need a recipe with ~0.3+ pp of margin, bought cheaply (e.g. via
progressive resizing savings reinvested in full-res epochs).
