### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.750, 0.757] (hit, 0.7523) but infeasible. Root cause: I extrapolated the first two 24->16 swaps'
cost (~0.15 pp each) to the third; the third cost ~0.3 pp. Plus the tip estimate (0.7554) left only ~0.25 pp margin.
Lesson: low-res swaps have diminishing returns; each additional early epoch at 16 px costs more than the previous.
