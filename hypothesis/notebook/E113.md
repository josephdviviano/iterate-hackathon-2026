H4.6 (open, c=0.55): Recipe re-draw noise. Any recipe change, even a math-neutral one, chaotically re-draws each seed's trajectory, with per-seed sd ~0.3-0.4 pp (E004/E011 moved the deterministic SGD 3-seed mean by 0.2-0.3 pp with zero process noise). More processes do not reduce this component; only more seeds do. With 22 seeds the SE of a recipe-vs-recipe difference is ~0.09-0.1 pp however many processes run. Untimed trials cost ~4.5 s, so a ~100-150-seed paired instrument (~10 min per arm) brings the SE to ~0.04 pp.
H4 (open, c=0.85): Accuracy noise sets the usable margin, and decisions near the gate select for luck. Three components. (a) Per-process cuDNN-pick divergence: the scored 3-seed mean varies ~0.2 pp across identical-code processes (E101: 0.7561 vs 0.7516), but the 22-seed off-seed process sd is only ~0.03 pp (E106). These disagree; see H4.5/H4.7. (b) Recipe re-draw: any recipe change chaotically re-draws each seed's trajectory (E004/E011 moved deterministic SGD means by 0.2-0.3 pp).
