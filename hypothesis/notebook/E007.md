### Outcome
accuracy 0.75017 (0.7515, 0.7499, 0.7491) vs 0.75493 at 8.5 ep: -0.48 pp; time 6.804 s vs 7.222 (-5.8%, as predicted
from steps). Infeasible -> discard. Verdict inconclusive (time as predicted; accuracy loss 0.48 pp just past the
0.15-0.4 pp "supports" band, short of the 0.6 pp refute bar). Local slope: ~0.95 pp per epoch at 8-8.5 epochs, i.e.
~0.08 pp per 1% of time. This is the exchange rate any time-saving idea must beat: E006's progressive resizing
(-0.69 pp for -18.6% = 0.037 pp per 1%) beats it by >2x, so resized epochs are a better use of compute than full-res ones.
This run is the control for I010 (brightness/contrast) and I011 (LS 0.2).

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.749, 0.755] (hit, 0.7502), time [6.74, 6.86] (hit, 6.804). Failure = infeasible and the
accuracy loss (0.48 pp per 0.5 epoch) was steeper than the 0.15-0.4 pp the idea expected. Root cause: the 8.5-epoch
reference sits on a steep part of the accuracy-vs-epochs curve for CIFAR-100 (~1 pp/epoch). Lesson: cutting
full-resolution epochs is the most expensive way to save time; every 1% of time saved must cost < 0.08 pp to beat it.
