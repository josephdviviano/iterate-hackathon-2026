H7 (supported, c=0.55): Optimizer quality per update was the binding constraint on CIFAR-100 at ~8 epochs (E008), and Muon on the 9 group 3x3 convs was the lever: +1.14 pp at equal epochs (E019), a flatter epoch slope (0.54 vs 0.95 pp/epoch, E020), and good large-batch tolerance (E022, E038). Muon's own knobs are now locally closed: momentum (E027, E030), lr (E034), NS count (E031, E033) and LS under Muon (E037) gave no gain >=0.2 pp. Further accuracy per epoch would need a different optimizer change (e.g. a per-neuron normalization like NorMuon, unproven on CNNs). Muon's per-step cost is cut only by fewer steps.
Idea: Explore a new accuracy source. On the E035 tip, move the classifier head (the linear 576->100 after the global max pool) from SGD to the compiled Muon update. Treat its weight as a [100, 576] matrix and use Muon lr scaled by sqrt(max(1, out/in)) as for the filters, with the same momentum and 3 NS steps. Leave the head's renorm/scale handling as in the SGD version and keep the bias on SGD. Add the shape to the compiled update and warm it up in build. CIFAR-100's 100-way head is a much larger share of the learning problem than CIFAR-10's 10-way head, and public Muon recipes tuned on CIFAR-10 never tried it.
Expected (from the queue): Time +0.3 to +1% (one more small NS matmul per step). Accuracy -0.4 to +0.4 pp vs the ~0.756 tip family. If the gain is >=0.25 pp (3-seed >=0.7585), it gives slack to spend on a 3rd 16 px epoch or a 20 px bridge. A loss means the head wants SGD's unnormalized, scale-sensitive updates (consistent with airbench), and the optimizer-assignment line is closed.
