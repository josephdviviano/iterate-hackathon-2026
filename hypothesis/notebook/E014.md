H2.5.1 (open, c=0.45): E006's 9.0-epoch schedule with 26 px instead of 24 px for the first phase, flip + translate-2 at 32 px followed by antialiased downsample (no random-resized crop), gives 3-seed accuracy >=0.752 (>= +0.4 pp over E006) at time <=6.3 s.
H2.5 (open, c=0.50): Most of E006's -0.69 pp came from its confounds, not low resolution itself: the 24 px stage gives a degenerate 2x2 map under the final 3x3 pool (24->23->11->5->2, while 26-32 px end at 3x3), and random-resized crop changes apparent object scale relative to test images (FixRes). A scale-preserving operator at >=26 px recovers most of the loss.
H2 (open, c=0.65): Per-epoch GPU compute drives the score. About 95% of GPU time is in conv, BN and activation kernels, and on this power-capped GPU time scales roughly with FLOPs/bytes. So cutting per-epoch FLOPs (lower early resolution, narrower high-resolution layers) reduces time, provided accuracy is recovered.
Idea: Take E006's schedule unchanged: 9.0 epochs, epochs 0-3 low-res, 4-6 at 28 px, 7-8 at 32 px. Make exactly two changes, which together are the H2.5 confound removal: (a) the first phase uses 26 px instead of 24 px, and (b) replace random-resized crop with flip + translate-2 at 32 px followed by an antialiased bilinear downsample to the phase resolution. Warm up all shapes in build.
Expected (from the queue): Time about 6.1-6.3 s, +4-6% vs E006's 5.88 s, since the 26 px epochs cost ~0.56 s vs 0.47 s. Accuracy 0.750-0.754, i.e. +0.2 to +0.6 pp over E006's 0.748. A gain of >=0.4 pp supports H2.5 (the loss came from confounds, not low resolution). A gain of <0.2 pp means the short full-res tail is the binding factor and favors the H2.1.2 schedule.

### Outcome

Verdict refutes H2.5.1/H2.5: replacing 24 px by 26 px (3x3 instead of 2x2 final map) gives no accuracy back while

Time matched the measured cost curve (pred ~6.34 + overheads; 6.49 actual).

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.746, 0.753] (hit), time [6.25, 6.45] (slight miss, 6.49: per-epoch switching overheads and
the fresh-shape first steps). The hypothesis that the 2x2 final map was "degenerate" was wrong: global max pooling
over 2x2 works as well as over 3x3. Lesson: for low-res phases pick resolution by the measured cost curve (24 px);
accuracy is governed by how much training happens at 32 px, not by the low-res geometry.
