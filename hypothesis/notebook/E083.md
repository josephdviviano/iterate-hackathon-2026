Replace the final exponential EMA with a LAWA-style uniform average: take a snapshot of the net weights every 4 steps over the last 20 steps (5 snapshots), keep them as a running mean via foreach_lerp with weight 1/k for the k-th snapshot, and evaluate on the mean with the BN buffers copied from the net.
