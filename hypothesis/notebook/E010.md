### Outcome
accuracy 0.7468 (0.7417, 0.7507, 0.7480) vs tip 0.75493: -0.81 pp; time 7.216 s vs 7.222 (-0.1%, i.e. unchanged) with

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.746, 0.755] (hit, at the bottom) and time [6.85, 7.25] (hit). The idea expected -3 to -6% from
fewer steps; the world model after E004 already predicted ~0-3%; the truth was 0%. Lesson: H3 (overhead) is
effectively dead on this hardware; batch size should be chosen for accuracy, not for speed. Larger batches also cost
accuracy at a fixed epoch budget here (-0.8 pp), so if anything smaller batches could buy accuracy for free.
