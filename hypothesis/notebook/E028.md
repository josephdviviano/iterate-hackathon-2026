## Details (full outcome and post-mortem, condensed above)

### Outcome
Scored identical rerun of the E026 tip: accuracy 0.7543 (0.7542, 0.7563, 0.7524) vs E026 0.75653 (0.7569, 0.7544,
0.7583); time 5.523 s vs 5.501 (+0.4%). NOT bit-identical: per-seed differences of -0.27, +0.19, -0.59 pp.
Side check before launch (untimed, harness --seed 3 --n 8, tip code): seeds 3-10 = 0.7552, 0.7547, 0.7541, 0.7536,
0.7573, 0.7554, 0.7595, 0.7524; mean 0.75528, std 0.22 pp; time 5.520 s.
Verdict refutes the pre-registered "bit-identical repeat" (E002 held for the SGD recipe, not for this one) -- the
seeds 3-10 part supports H4 (mean within 0.12 pp of seeds 0-2's E026 value, and 0.15 pp above E028's).
Best estimate of the tip's accuracy: seeds 0-2 averaged over E026+E028 = 0.7554; 11-seed mean ~0.7553.
So E026's 0.7565 was ~0.1 pp lucky; the real margin over 0.753 is ~0.25 pp, and a single 3-seed scored run of a
tip-like recipe has run-to-run noise of ~0.1-0.2 pp (sd) on its mean.
Likely cause: torch.compile max-autotune picks kernels by timing in each process; for the Muon update (bmm, Triton
reductions) the winner can differ between processes -> different reduction order -> chaotic divergence. (E001/E002
SGD runs compiled the net with the same mode and were bit-identical, so the net's picks were stable.)
Consequences: (1) accept small accuracy differences (< ~0.25 pp) as noise when comparing recipes now; (2) keep a
margin >= ~0.2 pp on seeds 0-2 to stay feasible across reruns; (3) the official 40-seed mean of the tip is ~0.755,
comfortably above 0.75.

### Post-mortem
Predicted bit-identical accuracy [0.7564, 0.7567]; got 0.7543. Root cause: the world model generalized E002's
determinism (SGD recipe) to the compiled-Muon recipe; max-autotune kernel selection for the Muon update (and/or
cuDNN benchmark picks for new shapes) varies per process, so runs diverge chaotically. Lesson: re-verify determinism
whenever compile targets or shapes change; until then, treat ~0.15 pp (sd) of run-to-run noise on the 3-seed mean as
part of every comparison, and keep >= 0.2 pp margin on kept tips.
