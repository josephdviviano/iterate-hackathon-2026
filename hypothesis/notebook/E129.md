# E129 · I138 · H2.20.5

H2.20.5 (open, c=0.25): FreezeOut-style refinement of the 85% freeze. (a) Anneal the group-1 and stem-BN LRs (Muon and SGD) to 0 over the ~10% of samples before the freeze point, instead of a hard detach at nonzero LR. (b) In the frozen window, put stem + group-1 BN in eval mode, a separate warmed static graph variant, which removes the batch-stat reductions over the 31x31x128 activations. Untimed, 100 seeds each vs the pooled E116 baseline. Prediction: (a) recovers >=0.04 pp of the ~0.09 pp freeze loss at equal time; (b) saves a further 0.2-0.5% at a loss <=0.02 pp. Either result lets the freeze point move earlier (e.g. 75-80%) at the same accuracy.
H2.20 (supported, c=0.65): Late layer freezing is a real rate lever with a small, real accuracy cost. Freezing stem + group 1 from 85% of samples saved ~4.5% at ~-0.09 pp vs the pooled 200-trajectory E100 baseline (E118: population mean 0.7521 over 198 trajectories), and the recipe is banked (E119). At 92% it saved ~2.5% at ~-0.02 pp (E120). That is ~0.02-0.03 pp per 1%, as good as or better than any measured trade. The loss grows faster than linearly as the freeze moves earlier. External priors: MosaicML gets ~5% with no Pareto gain. FreezeOut anneals each layer's LR to 0 at its freeze point and skips BN statistics in frozen layers, which E116 did not do. Open: the 75% point, a FreezeOut-style anneal + BN-eval refinement, and a deeper (group-2) freeze measured directly.
H2 (open, c=0.55): Per-epoch GPU work drives the score. On this machine, wall time is roughly proportional to conv FLOPs, except for widths that are not tile-aligned (E080: narrow group-2 widths recovered only 33-60% of their FLOP saving). Evidence for proportionality: E116's 15-20% cheaper frozen step matches its ~18.5% backward-FLOP removal once group-2 conv1's dgrad is counted. E117 (group-3 width) and E123 (depth) are also FLOP-proportional. The earlier 'bytes over FLOPs' core is demoted to H2.21, inconclusive. Tip: E119 (E116 code, stem + group 1 frozen from 85%), ~4.26 s, population mean ~0.7521. Live levers, all needing a large-seed accuracy test: depth cuts (E123: -7.6% for 2/3/3, -6.1% for 3/3/2), group-3 width 512 (E117: -5.2%), reverting the unvalidated bs-1024 late phases (~-2.5%), late-freeze refinements, 12 px swaps, and the 32 px trim rate, which is unmeasured and no longer listed as closed. Closed: CUDA graphs, Inductor tuning, cuDNN determinism/benchmark_limit, prepare, group-2 width, whitening-bias freeze point, 28 px substitution. Margin is gone (population 0.7521 vs the 0.7518 floor), so every accuracy-costing cut must be funded.
Idea: FreezeOut-style anneal before the 85% freeze, arm (a) only. Base: E116 code. Over 75%-85% of samples, ramp the LR multiplier of the group-1 filters (Muon) and the stem/group-1 BN params (SGD) linearly from 1 to 0, then detach at 85% exactly as now. Use separate 0-d LR tensors for the group-1 Muon stack and the group-1 SGD group, written host-side so nothing recompiles; if the group-1 filters currently share a stacked Muon update with others, scale their slice by a 0-d per-stack factor. All other groups, the schedule and the tail are unchanged. Time is unchanged. Accuracy is decided untimed on seeds 3-102 in one fresh process against the pooled ~198-trajectory E116 baseline.
Expected (from the queue): Time equal to the tip (±0.3%). Accuracy +0.02 to +0.06 pp over E116, recovering part of the ~0.09 pp freeze cost by removing the hard switch at a nonzero LR. A gain of >= +0.064 pp (2 SE) makes it a zero-time margin source that restores mu to ~E100's level. That would fund the bs-1536 revert or let the freeze move to ~80%. A result within ±0.03 pp means the freeze cost is lost late adaptation, not the discontinuity, and arm (b) (BN eval in the frozen window) becomes the only remaining refinement.
