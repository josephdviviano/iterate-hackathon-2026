### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.747, 0.754] (hit). The premise (short schedules benefit from annealing to 0) does not hold with a
lookahead/EMA that already averages the late iterates. Lesson: like LS (E009) and augmentation (E008), the reference's

null result -> stop spending runs on reference-hparam retuning.
