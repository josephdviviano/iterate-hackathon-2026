# E018 · I020 · H6.2.1

H6.2.1 (open, c=0.30): On a progressive schedule (E006 or its successor), a forward-only BN re-estimation pass at 32 px over ~10% of an epoch of augmented training data, run inside train, raises 3-seed accuracy by >=0.2 pp at <2% time cost.
H6.2 (open, c=0.35): After progressive resizing, BN statistics and the EMA weights are dominated by low-res epochs. Re-estimating BN statistics at 32 px on training data at the end of train (FixRes-style) removes the train/test resolution mismatch cheaply.
H6 (open, c=0.45): Zero-time-cost accuracy levers (LR schedule shape, EMA/label-smoothing settings, BN recalibration at the eval resolution) can buy 0.2-0.7 pp of accuracy slack. That slack can then be spent on epoch or resolution cuts, which have a known time payoff (~0.85 s/epoch, ~-18% for progressive resizing).
Idea: On E006's code (commit ed8dc49), add only a FixRes-style BN re-estimation at the end of train. After the final EMA/lookahead weights are set, reset the BN running stats and run a forward-only pass in train mode (momentum=None, cumulative average) at 32 px over ~5000 augmented training images (5 batches of 1024, flip + translate only), under no_grad. Warm up this shape in build (it is the existing 32 px train shape).
Expected (from the queue): Time +0.5 to +1.5% (about 5.92-5.97 s). Accuracy +0.0 to +0.3 pp over E006's 0.748. A small gain is likely because E006 already ended with 2 epochs at 32 px. A gain of >=0.2 pp supports H6.2.1 and makes it a cheap add-on for any schedule with a short full-res tail.

## Details (full outcome and post-mortem, condensed above)

### Outcome
accuracy 0.74457 (0.7463, 0.7436, 0.7438) vs E006 0.7480: -0.34 pp; time 5.947 s vs 5.880 (+1.1%). Verdict refutes
H6.2.1. Contingency "opposite way": re-estimating BN statistics as a cumulative average over 5 x 1024 fresh 32 px
batches is worse than the running stats left by training (bn_momentum 0.6, i.e. a fast EMA over the last ~few
32 px steps, consistent with the lookahead weights). FixRes-style re-estimation is not needed when the schedule ends
at full resolution; possibly the lookahead-averaged weights pair better with the training-time running stats.

### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.745, 0.752] (miss low: 0.7446). The idea assumed E006's BN stats were contaminated by low-res epochs;
with momentum 0.4 per step, the stats after 96 full-res steps contain essentially no low-res information, so there
was nothing to fix, and replacing them with a 5-batch cumulative estimate only added noise/mismatch. Lesson: check
the BN momentum before assuming stale statistics; with bn_momentum 0.6 the running stats reflect the last ~5 steps.
