## Outcome
<!-- After the run: the numbers, which contingency fired, the verdict and why. -->
Renorm target decaying linearly to 0.157x of sqrt(fan_out) with the LR unchanged: scored 0.4874, off-seed 0.5081 -> training collapses. Refutes H9.1.1 as specified: with Muon's fixed-size updates (lr * orthogonal U, ~0.24 per unit-norm filter row at peak), shrinking filter norms 6x makes the relative step ~6x larger. hiverge's norm decay must come with a correspondingly smaller Muon LR (the relative step lr/||w|| is the real knob). A follow-up would decay the target AND scale lr by the same factor (constant relative step) or schedule lr/||w|| directly.
