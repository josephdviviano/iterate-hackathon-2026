# E022 · I024 · H1.6

H1.6 (open, c=0.35): On this energy-bound GPU the optimizer step count is nearly free in time (E010: half the steps, same time), and more updates help accuracy at a fixed epoch budget (E010: bs 2000 cost -0.81 pp). Smaller batches (512-768) should therefore buy accuracy at roughly equal time, unless smaller GEMMs lose efficiency (E012).
H1 (open, c=0.60): Sample efficiency at full resolution drives the score. A1a (airbench96-style: frozen 2x2 whitening stem, 3-conv groups 128/384/576 with dirac init and residuals, SGD-Nesterov lr 9 with BN biases at 64x LR, LS 0.3, lookahead-EMA, alternating flip + translate 2) reaches >=75.3% at 8.5 epochs (E001/E002: 0.7549, 7.22 s), but its margin is thin: neutral perturbations land at 0.752-0.753 (E004, E011). The local slope is steep, ~1 pp/epoch at 8.0-8.5 (E007). At 8.0 full-res epochs, zero-cost augmentation, LS and LR-endpoint tweaks showed no effect >=0.3 pp (E008, E009, E015). New accuracy slack must therefore come from cheaper epochs, more updates or capacity per FLOP, not from retuning the reference.
Idea: On E019's Muon recipe at 8.5 epochs, raise the batch size from 1024 to 2000. Scale the SGD param groups (BN/bias/head) by the same kilostep rule as E010, and scale the EMA/lookahead schedule by the step count. Keep Muon lr 0.24 and momentum 0.6, which are airbench94_muon's own settings at bs 2000. Warm up the new shapes in build. This tests whether Muon removes the large-batch penalty SGD showed (E010: -0.81 pp) while halving the per-step Muon overhead.
Expected (from the queue): Time 7.55-7.70 s (E019's 8.04 s minus half of the ~0.82 s Muon overhead; the conv work is unchanged per E010). Accuracy 0.756-0.766: a smaller penalty than SGD's -0.8 pp if Muon tolerates large batches. A loss of <=0.3 pp makes bs 2000 the preferred Muon batch, since it halves the optimizer overhead. A loss of >=0.7 pp confirms that updates per epoch are binding (H1.6) for both optimizers.

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.752, 0.765] (hit) and time [7.45, 7.75] (hit). Logged as a failure only because it is
not an improvement on the tip (it was run on the 8.5-epoch E019 base by design). The world model's H1.6 ("more updates
help at fixed epochs", from SGD in E010) did not transfer to Muon: Muon's orthogonalized, normalized updates make the
step count matter much less. Lesson: Muon changes which knobs are binding -- batch size is now a nearly free lever for
cutting Muon overhead, and SGD-era conclusions about step counts must be re-checked under Muon.
