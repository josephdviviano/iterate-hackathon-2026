Mechanisms: fewer early steps (large batch, sample-indexed warmup) and noisier or lower-information early inputs (16 px) both hurt the same fragile early phase. hiverge and Fable train the whitening bias only for the first 20% of steps, which supports the bias-LR channel.
H8 (open, c=0.20): Early-training fragility: in the ~256-step regime, the quantity and quality of early updates might bound final accuracy. Re-baselined, its pillars weaken. Only E032 (no 24 px bridge, -0.68 pp) is large. Treat as a weak prior: avoid levers that coarsen early training, but do not spend runs testing it directly unless a lever doubles as a time saving (e.g. whitening-bias freeze).
