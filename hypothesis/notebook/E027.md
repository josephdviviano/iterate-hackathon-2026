## Details (full outcome and post-mortem, condensed above)

### Outcome
accuracy 0.74477 (0.7455, 0.7469, 0.7419) vs tip 0.75653: -1.18 pp; time 5.522 s (= tip). Verdict refutes: Muon
momentum 0.85 is much worse than 0.6 at bs 1024 on this recipe. Contingency "opposite way" fired. The loss is large
(~1.2 pp, ~4x the perturbation noise), so momentum is a sensitive knob -- worth checking the other direction
(0.5 / 0.45) and Muon lr (0.16 / 0.32) next, since 0.6/0.24 were tuned on CIFAR-10 at bs 2000 without lookahead.

### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.751, 0.761] -> miss low (0.7448). The idea's premise (smaller batch -> favors higher momentum) ignored that
this recipe already has a lookahead-EMA and Nesterov on top; with Muon's normalized updates, higher momentum makes
effective steps larger and smoother late in a short schedule, apparently overshooting. Lesson: Muon hyperparameters
are sensitive here; tune them in the direction away from the failure (lower momentum) before assuming optimality.
