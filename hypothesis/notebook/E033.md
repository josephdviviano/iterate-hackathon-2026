## Details (full outcome and post-mortem, condensed above)

### Outcome
accuracy 0.7543 (0.7497, 0.7590, 0.7542) vs tip estimate 0.7554: -0.11 pp (noise); time 5.638 s vs 5.501: +2.5%
(+0.137 s, one NS iteration as measured in E031). Verdict refutes H7 for the iteration count: 3 iterations are
saturated; 4 buys nothing. Contingency "opposite way": NS line closed in both directions (2: -1.8 pp; 4: +0).
Follow-up analysis (untimed, after the run): captured the actual Muon inputs (Nesterov momentum-gradient matrices,
Frobenius-normalized) for all 9 filters at steps 5/50/120/200/300/380 of a tip trial: 19,584 singular values with
quantiles 1%: 0.0004, 10%: 0.0064, 50%: 0.0215, 90%: 0.070, 99%: 0.20 (heavy-tailed, low-rank-ish, not Gaussian).
Fraction of output singular values in [0.7, 1.3] / mean |1-s|:
  fixed quintic x2: 0.12 / 0.673 (E031: -1.8 pp) | x3: 0.56 / 0.334 (tip) | x4: 0.83 / 0.205 (E033: +0 pp)
  Polar-Express 3-step, l=1e-3: 0.21 / 0.563 | l=3e-3: 0.30 / 0.431 | l=1e-2: 0.83 / 0.244 (max 1.27).
So accuracy saturates once ~half the spectrum is near 1; the 3-step Polar coefficients with l=1e-3 are WORSE than the
fixed ones on these spectra, and l=1e-2 only matches x4, which gave nothing. Coefficient tuning cannot help.

### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.753, 0.761] (hit, 0.7543) but no gain from the 4th NS iteration. Root cause: E031 (2 -> 3 =
+1.8 pp) suggested a steep quality-accuracy curve; the spectrum analysis shows 3 iterations already bring ~56% of the
real spectrum near 1 and the remainder (tiny singular values) matters little. Lesson: accuracy saturates in
orthogonalization quality; measure the actual spectra before extrapolating a steep local slope.
