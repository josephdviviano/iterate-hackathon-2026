# E047 · I055 · H2.10

H2.10 (open, c=0.30): Under Muon the 32 px tail is binding: one 32->24 epoch swap costs ~0.5 pp (E025). Step-indexed fractional switch points would let schedules land just above the threshold instead of moving in whole-epoch (0.3-0.85 s) chunks. Exchange-rate arithmetic is unfavourable for 'swap early epochs to 16 px and top up the 32 px tail': a third 16 px epoch saves 0.256 s for ~0.3-0.4 pp, and spending 0.256 s on ~0.3 extra 32 px epochs buys only ~0.15 pp at ~0.5 pp/epoch. The fractional knob is mainly useful for fine trimming once margin exists.
H2 (open, c=0.70): Per-epoch GPU work and per-step overhead drive the score. The strongest schedule lever is cheap early epochs: 2@16 + 2@24 + 4@32 px (E026), and 24 px is the minimum useful bridge (E032, E041). Measured epoch cost relative to 32 px: 16 px 0.32x, 20 px ~0.51x, 24 px 0.57x, 26 px 0.75x, 28 px 0.79x, 30 px 0.95x (E013, E026, E041). Narrowing (E017) and widening (E005) were refuted only under SGD. In the low-res phases a per-step cost (~2 ms/step at bs 1024, E038) is a large share, which is why bs 1536 paid off. Every Muon schedule so far kept 8 total epochs and a single global batch size.
Idea: On the E044 tip, cut half of the 24 px phase using a step-indexed switch: 2@16 + 1.5@24 + 4@32 px, 7.5 total epochs. Rescale the LR warmup/decay, EMA, lookahead and renorm schedules to the new total step count, the same way the recipe already does for epoch changes. Change nothing else, and use only shapes that are already warmed up. This tests whether a fractional removal of a cheap mid-resolution epoch, which also removes Muon steps, trades better than swapping 24 px for 16 px.
Expected (from the queue): Time about 4.96-5.01 s (-4.5 to -5.5%): half a 24 px epoch (~0.25 s) plus ~0.02 s of Muon/per-step cost. Accuracy 0.7540-0.7560, i.e. -0.15 to -0.35 pp vs 0.7574 (Muon slope ~0.54 pp/epoch, a 24 px epoch worth ~half a 32 px one). If the loss is <=0.25 pp, the exchange rate is <=0.05 pp per 1% and fractional trimming becomes the fine-tuning knob for the remaining margin. A loss of >=0.4 pp means the 24 px bridge is binding at its current length.
