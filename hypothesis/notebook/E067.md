# E067 · I073 · H2.16.1

H2.16.1 (open, c=0.45): Untimed profiler breakdown on the E056 base at 32 px, bs 1536. Per conv layer per step, the BN/GELU forward and backward make >=4 full read/write passes over the fp16 activation, and the elementwise/reduction kernels take >=25% of GPU time. If they take <15% or already run at <=2 passes, the lever is dead and N_elem is refuted.
H2.16 (open, c=0.35): The fused BN/GELU/elementwise Triton kernels take ~30% of GPU time in every phase (E052). They are memory-traffic and energy bound, and nothing has targeted them yet. Fewer passes over each fp16 activation (BN stats + apply + GELU fused in forward; recompute-friendly fused backward) would cut bytes moved at identical math. That saving is energy-proportional, so it should help both the capped 24/32 px phases and the 300 W PCIe judge. Try Inductor-level options first (fusion/tuning flags, warmed up in build), then a hand-written Triton kernel.
H2 (open, c=0.65): Per-epoch GPU work drives the score. Tip family (E046 code: bs 1536, 3@16 + 1@24 + 4@32 px, 8.0 ep, compiled Muon on 9 convs + head): ~4.95 s, but its pooled accuracy over 5 same-math runs (E046, E048, E052, E053, E056) is only 0.7533, which is at the gate. Two math-equivalent speedups are measured but not banked: static per-resolution graphs (-2.0%, E053) and a global max via flatten(2).max(dim=2).values instead of AdaptiveMaxPool2d(1) (another -2.9%, E056), together 4.718 s. Banking them needs about +0.1-0.2 pp of accuracy at zero time cost. Cost structure: the 32 px tail is ~71% of time and energy-bound at the 400 W cap. 16 px is low-occupancy (98% GPU-busy, E052), not launch-bound, so CUDA graphs and launch levers are closed. Remaining time levers, ranked: (a) math-neutral kernel efficiency (fused BN/GELU elementwise, ~30% of GPU time, E052) and FLOP removal at no accuracy cost (whitening-bias freeze removes the group-1 conv1 dgrad); (b) equal-time schedule reshuffles (E047); (c) width cuts, only after a per-layer profile; (d) fractional trims, only once accuracy slack exists.
