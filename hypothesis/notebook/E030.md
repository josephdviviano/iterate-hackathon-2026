### Post-mortem
Predicted [0.752, 0.760] (hit, 0.7550); no gain. The hypothesis (lower momentum helps, given E027's failure
upward) was plausible, but Muon momentum turned out flat in 0.5-0.6. Lesson: a sharp loss on one side of a
hyperparameter does not imply a gain on the other side; tune only when there is a mechanism, not by symmetry.
