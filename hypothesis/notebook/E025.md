# E025 · I028 · H2.7

H2.7 (open, c=0.50): Accuracy at a fixed short budget tracks total training epochs/steps far more than the share spent at 32 px: a 24 px epoch carries most of a 32 px epoch's accuracy value at 57% of its cost. Evidence pattern: E013 put 1.8x more epochs at 32 px than E006 yet gained only +0.26 pp, and E006 (9 ep, mostly low-res) is only 0.22 pp behind 8 full-res epochs (E007) while costing 0.92 s less.
H2 (open, c=0.70): Per-epoch GPU energy (FLOPs + bytes) drives the score on this power-capped GPU: only cutting GPU work shortens training (E004, E010, E011). Lower-resolution epochs are the strongest lever so far (E006: -18.6% time for -0.69 pp, 0.037 pp per 1% of time, about 2x better than cutting full-res epochs). Measured epoch cost is stepwise in resolution (24 px 57%, 26 px 75%, 28 px 79%, 30 px 95% of a 32 px epoch; E013), so the levers are 24 px (or lower) phases and narrower high-FLOP layers, with the saved time reinvested in more total epochs.
Idea: On the E021 tip, change only the resolution split: 5 epochs at 24 px + 3 epochs at 32 px (was 4 + 4), still 8.0 total. Keep the E006 operator (flip + translate-2 at 32 px, antialiased bilinear downsample), AdaptiveMaxPool2d(1), eager Muon, and LR/EMA/lookahead rescaled to the step count, which does not change. The compiled shapes are already warmed up in build.

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.753, 0.759] (hit) and time [5.58, 5.72] (hit). The hypothesis (loss <= 0.3 pp) failed: with only
3 epochs at 32 px the network loses ~0.5 pp. Lesson: under Muon, the value of a 32 px epoch relative to a 24 px epoch is
~2x, matching its ~1.75x cost, so shifting the split is roughly cost-neutral in exchange-rate terms; the remaining big
levers are optimizer quality per epoch and per-epoch cost at fixed resolution.
