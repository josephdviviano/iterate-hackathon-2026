# E035 · I044 · H7.2.1

H7.2.1 (open, c=0.35): On the E026 tip, renormalizing Muon weights every 2 + int(15*progress) steps instead of every step (inside the compiled update, with the step flag passed as a tensor so nothing recompiles) cuts time by >=1.5% with a 3-seed accuracy >=0.7535.
H7.2 (open, c=0.50): The Muon overhead left after E024 (~1 ms/step x 384 steps, ~0.4 s, ~7%) can be cut at near-equal math. Options: renormalize weights every 2->17 steps instead of every step (hiverge, Fable), use 2 Newton-Schulz steps with better coefficients (Polar Express, Turbo-Muon AOL), or use a larger batch. The cost is fixed per step, so it is a larger share of the 16/24 px epochs.
H7 (open, c=0.75): Optimizer quality per update is the binding constraint on CIFAR-100 at ~8 epochs (the short schedule under-fits, E008). Muon on the 9 group 3x3 convs is the main lever: +1.14 pp at equal epochs (E019), a flatter accuracy-vs-epochs slope (0.54 vs 0.95 pp/epoch, E020), and tolerance of large batches (E022: -0.25 pp at bs 2000 vs SGD's -0.81). Its hyperparameters are sensitive (E027: momentum 0.85 cost 1.18 pp) and were tuned for CIFAR-10 at bs 2000 without lookahead, so both accuracy per epoch and Muon's remaining per-step cost (~1 ms/step, ~7% of time) still have room.
Idea: On the E026 tip, change only how often the Muon-managed conv filters are renormalized: every 2 + int(15*progress) steps instead of every step (hiverge/Fable style). Do it inside the compiled update with a 0-d bool/int tensor flag (torch.where-gated, or two warmed-up variants selected on the host) so nothing recompiles in timed code. Warm up both paths in build. Everything else is unchanged. Before scoring, log per-step update time with CUDA events in an untimed run, to separate the renorm cost from NS.
Expected (from the queue): Time -0.5 to -2% (about 5.39-5.47 s). Renorm is a norm plus a scale over 9 filters, probably ~0.05-0.15 ms/step, so the >=1.5% that H7.2.1 predicts is optimistic. Accuracy within ±0.25 pp of 0.7554. If time falls by <0.7%, renorm is not a meaningful part of the Muon overhead, and what remains is NS matmuls plus momentum traffic, which only batch size can cut.
