## Details (full outcome and post-mortem, condensed above)

### Outcome
accuracy 0.74887 (0.7500, 0.7483, 0.7483) vs 0.75493 for GELU: -0.61 pp. time 7.061 s vs 7.218 s: -2.2%.
Both predictions in range, but the accuracy loss (> 0.2 pp) refutes H2.2.1 and makes it infeasible -> discard.
Exchange rate: 2.2% time for 0.6 pp accuracy is a bad trade (0.6 pp likely needs ~0.5-1 extra epoch = 6-12% time).
Activation choice matters for accuracy on CIFAR-100; GELU stays. Contingency: "opposite way" for accuracy.
Side finding (profiling between runs): prepare's ~65 ms steady state is ~45 ms nn.init.dirac_, which does ~2700
per-element host->device copies with stream syncs; H2D upload 7 ms, normalize 5.5 ms, whitening 1.7 ms, pad 3 ms.

### Post-mortem
Predicted accuracy [0.748, 0.757] and time [6.95, 7.25]; both hit, but the hypothesis (SiLU is free) failed:
-0.61 pp. Root cause: the world model treated the activation as a pure cost knob; on CIFAR-100 at ~8 epochs GELU's
smoother gradient seems to matter for sample efficiency. Also the time saving (-2.2%) was real even though the GPU is
power-capped: GELU's erf costs energy in the fused BN+act kernels. Lesson: elementwise ops are not free on a
power-capped GPU, but accuracy changes dominate the exchange rate (0.6 pp ~ 7.5% of time at the epoch slope).
