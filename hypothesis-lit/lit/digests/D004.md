# D004: Which training-recipe changes raise CIFAR-100 (or CIFAR-10) test accuracy at a fixed short training budget (<=15 epochs) with no extra compute: e.g. label smoothing level, BatchNorm variants or zero-init, activation choice, head/pooling design, weight decay or momentum settings, data ordering, augmentation strength, init schemes?

Request R004 (agent, after E034); 80 papers found, 6 relevant, 5 read in full, 17 verified claims; cost $0.91

## Digest: zero-compute accuracy knobs for short CIFAR training

**Bottom line:** Most verified cards describe airbench (CIFAR-10) components the recipe already has. They account for where the recipe came from, but they don't point to new margin. None of them is a direct CIFAR-100 ≤15-epoch ablation. Margin is now the binding constraint: E033 scored 0.7531 and its salt-1 companion E034 scored 0.7518, a pooled mean of about 0.7525, which is below 0.753. A few untested knobs that add no compute are worth one run each.

**Already in the recipe (explains):**
- **C0019** (patch whitening), **C0020** (Dirac init), **C0021** (BN-bias LR scaling) and **C0022** (GELU) have all been in place since E001.
- **C0025** (alternating flip, a 27% effective speedup at 20 epochs on CIFAR-10) is also already on. Don't spend a run on H1.2.1. Treat it as literature-supported and move on.
- **C0027**: airbench uses label smoothing 0.2 on 10 classes. This loop found 0.3 ≥ 0.2 on 100 classes (E012), and lowering it in the low-res phase didn't help (E026). That fits H1.3: more classes favour more smoothing. The unexplored direction is upward. Try ls 0.35–0.4 at fixed epochs, with 2 salts.
- **C0028** (multi-crop TTA) is banned here, so it is irrelevant.

**Conditional (shown in other regimes, untested here):**
- **C0021 under Muon:** the 64× BN-bias multiplier was tuned for Nesterov SGD. Under Muon plus Lookahead it was never re-swept. Try 0.5× and 2× on the SGD-group multiplier.
- **C0031 and C0035, BN statistics:** the gains come from fixing a mismatch between train-time and test-time BN statistics (dropout variance in C0035: up to 2 pp on CIFAR-100 with DenseNet; LR-dependent momentum in C0031). Here the analogous shift comes from progressive resizing and the frozen or detached early block (E029, E033). This is the mechanism behind H2.3.2.
  - **Action:** at the end of `train`, re-estimate BN running stats on a few un-augmented 32 px training batches, forward only. That costs about 0.03–0.05 s.
  - **Alternative:** raise the BN momentum only in the 32 px phase.
  - Either test is a direct probe of H2.3.2. The weight EMA in E019 failed, but it didn't target BN stats from the current weights.
- **C0029/C0030, cyclical momentum:** momentum lowered while the LR is high was worth +1.2 pp on ResNet-56 with SGD. Muon momentum is fixed at 0.655 here. Cycling it (for example 0.6 at peak, 0.7 at the ends) is free, because the schedule tensor is already on the GPU. Untested; one run with 2 salts.

**Untested mechanisms the tree lacks:**
- **C0024:** the head output scale (airbench uses 1/9) was only rescaled for 100 classes, never swept. It interacts with ls 0.3. Sweep 0.5×/2× as a free sibling of H1.3.
- **C0026:** check that the 2 px translate uses reflection padding, not zero padding, before downscaling. This is a code check, not a run.
- **C0023:** dropping the BN affine weight saves a little backward work and an optimizer group. The effect is small, and the change pairs naturally with H2.4.

**Not applicable:** C0032–C0034 (decorrelated BN, group whitening) add compute per step and were tested on MLPs or VGG at long schedules. Skip them.

**Priority for margin:**
1. BN re-estimation or BN momentum at 32 px.
2. Head-scale sweep.
3. Label smoothing 0.35.
4. Cyclical Muon momentum.

Judge each against the pooled salt estimate. A single draw can't resolve deltas under about 0.3 pp (H4).

## Claims
- C0019 (explains; H1.1): Patch whitening is already in the recipe (E001). No action.
- C0020 (explains; H1.1): Dirac init is already in the recipe. No action.
- C0021 (conditional; H1.7): The 64x BN-bias LR was tuned under SGD and never re-swept under Muon+Lookahead. Sweep the multiplier at 0.5x/2x with 2 salts.
- C0022 (explains; H1.1): GELU is already used. No action.
- C0023 (untested; H2.4): Dropping the BN affine weight is a small cut in backward work and optimizer groups. Low priority.
- C0024 (untested; H1.3): The head output scale was never swept for 100 classes with ls 0.3. Run a free 0.5x/2x sweep to buy margin.
- C0025 (explains; H1.2, H1.2.1): Alternating flip is already on. Treat H1.2 as literature-supported and don't spend a run on H1.2.1.
- C0026 (untested; H1.3): Code-check that the translate uses reflection padding before downscaling. Fix it if it uses zero padding.
- C0027 (explains; H1.3.1): CIFAR-10 uses ls 0.2. Here 0.3 >= 0.2 (E012, E026). The untested direction is upward: try ls 0.35-0.4.
- C0029 (conditional; H1.7): Super-convergence with cyclical LR is the basis of the triangular schedule already in use. It informs the cyclical-momentum test.
- C0030 (conditional; H1.7, H1.7.6): Cyclical momentum (lower at peak LR) helped SGD on ResNet-56. Test cycling Muon momentum 0.6-0.7 via the GPU schedule tensor, at zero cost.
- C0031 (conditional; H2.3.2): Fast BN momentum is needed under large LR, and resolution changes shift BN statistics. Raise BN momentum in the 32 px phase as a probe of H2.3.2.
- C0035 (conditional; H2.3.2, H4.2): Re-estimating BN stats fixes a train/test statistics mismatch (up to 2 pp on CIFAR-100). Re-estimate on a few un-augmented 32 px batches at the end of train (~0.05 s). Top priority for margin.
- C0032 (untested; H1.1): Decorrelated BN adds per-step compute, so it is out of scope for a no-extra-compute budget. Skip.
- C0028 (untested; H1.1.2): TTA is banned by RULES. Irrelevant.
