# E026 · I031 · H2.3

H2.3 (open, c=0.55): Progressive resizing (whole-image bilinear downsampling to 24 then 28 px in early epochs, then 32 px) cuts step cost at little accuracy cost. This applies especially here, where step cost concentrates in the high-resolution group-1 layers.
H2 (open, c=0.65): Time is mainly set by per-step throughput: how fast each training step runs on the A100 (precision, memory layout, compilation, data pipeline, FLOPs per step), independent of how many steps are needed.
Idea: Explore, following C0012 (EfficientNetV2: scale regularization with image size). On E021 at a fixed epoch schedule, weaken regularization only in the 24 px phase. Use label smoothing 0.2 instead of 0.3 and crop translation of 1 px instead of 2, applied before downscaling, during epochs 1-5. The 32 px phase stays at ls 0.3 and 2 px translate. Pass the ls value through the CUDA graph as a GPU scalar tensor, or capture the low-res graph with its own constant, so nothing is recaptured in the timed region. Time should be unchanged.
Expected (from the queue): Time unchanged (±0.5%). Accuracy +0.1 to +0.25 pp, to about 0.756-0.758. Weaker regularization should let the short, under-trained low-res phase fit faster, while the full-res phase still applies ls 0.3. Since the E020/E021 trajectory noise is ~0.15-0.2 pp, only a gain >=0.2 pp counts as support. In that case, spend it on -0.25 full-res epoch (about -0.2 s). A drop of >=0.15 pp refutes the per-phase-regularization idea here.
