## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.7545, 0.7600] (+0.35-0.6 pp); got 0.7527 (-0.06 pp). The world model assumed the ~0.9 pp/epoch slope measured at 9.0->9.5 full-res epochs (E009->E014, 2/3/3 without resizing) still holds at the end of the 11-epoch resizing recipe. Two explanations, not separable with one run: (a) saturation: with resizing the recipe already has ~9.5 full-res-equivalent epochs plus a long LR tail, and the marginal 32 px epoch is worth much less than at 9 epochs; (b) noise: per-seed std is ~0.4 pp (E025/E027), so a 3-seed delta has ~0.33 pp SE for *differences* between configs, and E020->E021 (-0.36 pp for -0.25 ep) and E030->E032 (-0.06 pp for +0.5 ep) are both within ~1 SE of a ~0.5 pp/epoch slope. What the world model got wrong: it treated 3-seed deltas of 0.3-0.4 pp as readable. Lesson: at this stage, single 3-seed comparisons cannot resolve effects below ~0.5 pp; margin decisions need either 2 salts (6 draws) or larger effects. Do not plan to buy margin with +0.25-0.5 epoch increments on faith.
