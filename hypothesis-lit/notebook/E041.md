## Outcome
<!-- After the run: the numbers, which contingency fired, the verdict and why. -->
CRASH: trial 1 returned non-finite logits ("Classifier returned nonfloating or nonfinite logits"). Diagnosis (dev): with torch.compile, the backward of x.amax(dim=(2,3)) inside the full net produced NaN grads already in the first epoch (eager was fine; amax compiled in isolation was fine). Likely cause: inductor's min-cut partitioner recomputes the BN/GELU output in the backward at a different precision, so the tie mask (x == amax) can be all-false and the gradient is divided by a zero tie count. Fix: x.flatten(2).max(dim=2).values (backward via saved indices), verified finite under compile at 20 and 32 px. Contingency 'crash': dumb, fixable bug -> fix, commit, re-run the same idea.
