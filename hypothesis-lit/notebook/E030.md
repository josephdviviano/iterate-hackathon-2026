# E030 · I037 · H2.3

H2.3 (supported, c=0.70): Progressive resizing (whole-image bilinear downsampling to 24 then 28 px in early epochs, then 32 px) cuts step cost at little accuracy cost. This applies especially here, where step cost concentrates in the high-resolution group-1 layers.
H2 (open, c=0.72): Time is mainly set by per-step throughput: how fast each training step runs on the A100 (precision, memory layout, compilation, data pipeline, FLOPs per step), independent of how many steps are needed.
Idea: Exploit the resolution ladder one rung lower. On E025, run epochs 1-2 at 20 px instead of 24 px, so the schedule becomes 2x20 + 3x24 + 2x28 + 4x32 (11 epochs in total, the same LR schedule over total steps). Use whole augmented images downscaled with bilinear F.interpolate after the 2 px translate crop, as for 24/28 px, and keep the AdaptiveMaxPool2d(1) head. Batch 1536 with drop_last. Capture and warm up a 4th CUDA graph (20 px) in build. Rationale: the first epochs learn coarse features and the triangle is still warming up, so resolution matters least there. E020 and E025 both gained from cheaper early pixels.
Expected (from the queue): A 20 px epoch costs about 0.40-0.44 s versus about 0.54 s at 24 px, so time falls about 0.2-0.28 s (-3 to -4%, to about 7.06-7.14 s). Accuracy falls 0.05-0.3 pp, centred near -0.15 pp (about 0.752 expected). If the 3-seed mean is >=0.753, keep it. If it falls between 0.750 and 0.753, combine it with any free margin gain (the Muon lr idea) or add +0.25 epoch at 32 px; the net should still be about -0.1 s. If it falls below 0.750, the low-res floor is 24 px.
