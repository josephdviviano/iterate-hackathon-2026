H1.6.1 (open, c=0.30): Prediction: dropping the 25% lowest-loss examples in the last half of training cuts train time by >=10%, with 3-seed mean accuracy falling by <=0.2 pp.
H1.6 (open, c=0.30): Skipping low-loss (easy) examples in later epochs, selected from losses computed during training at no extra cost, cuts the number of steps without losing accuracy.
H1 (open, c=0.80): Time is mainly set by sample efficiency: how many epochs/steps the recipe needs to reach >=75.3% (architecture, init, optimizer, schedule, label smoothing, augmentation). Recipes that need fewer epochs dominate the score.
Idea: Explore. Add loss-based data filtering. During the second half of training, keep the per-example loss computed in the forward pass. Each epoch, drop the 25% lowest-loss examples from the previous epoch, so second-half epochs have fewer batches. Batches stay fixed at 1024 with drop_last, so no new shapes appear. The losses are a byproduct of training (no extra forward passes), and the selection runs on the GPU with the trial-seeded generator. Keep the LR schedule defined over the total number of steps.
Expected (from the queue): Train time -10-13% (about -0.9 to -1.1 s). Accuracy falls by 0.1-0.6 pp. H1.6.1 holds if the fall is <=0.2 pp. Even a 0.3-0.5 pp fall is a net win at the ~1 pp/s exchange rate, if spending the saved time on epochs recovers more than that.
