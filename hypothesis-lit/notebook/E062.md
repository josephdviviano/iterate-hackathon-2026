# E062 · I069 · H2.4

H2.4 (supported, c=0.65): Work removal at high resolution: backward work through the earliest, fastest-converging layers (whitening-output dgrad, the first group-1 conv's wgrad and its BN/GELU/pool backward) carries little accuracy once those layers have trained in the low-res phases. Stopping backward there (detach or freeze, FreezeOut-style) cuts time at no measurable accuracy cost. Group-1 depth itself cannot go below 2 convs (E023).
