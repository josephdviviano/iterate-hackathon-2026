## Outcome
<!-- After the run: the numbers, which contingency fired, the verdict and why. -->
Official 0.7335, 6.72 s; dev seeds 3-12: 0.7327 (-2.1 pp vs 0.7534). Verdict: refutes H1.7.9 decisively. Contingency: discard, close H1.7.9. Mechanism: Polar Express' per-iteration polynomials are designed to be composed over the full schedule; truncated after 2 iterations they map the spectrum into [~0.2, ~2] (median 1.0-1.7), i.e. a very uneven, over-scaled update — worse than the fixed quintic. Lifting the small-σ tail (the offline criterion) is not sufficient; the update's spectral flatness/overall scale matters. Together with E035 (1 NS step: -1.8 pp), Muon accuracy is very sensitive to the orthogonalization quality at this budget; the incumbent's AOL + 2 fixed quintic steps should be treated as a floor.
