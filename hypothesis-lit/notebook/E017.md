## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted time [8.45, 8.80]; got 8.41. I assumed +1-3% from more Newton-Schulz calls and smaller GEMMs (304 vs 238 steps). Actual per-image cost was unchanged. Root cause: at batch >= 1536 the per-step fixed cost (Muon NS on 8 filters, optimizer launches, Lookahead) is small relative to the ~27 ms of convolution compute, and conv efficiency does not drop between 2000 and 1536. Lesson: batch size down to ~1536 is time-neutral per epoch; the step-count overhead only matters at much smaller batches.
