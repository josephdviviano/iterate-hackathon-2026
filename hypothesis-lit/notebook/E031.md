## Outcome
<!-- After the run: the numbers, which contingency fired, the verdict and why. -->
accuracy 0.7489 (0.7516 / 0.7447 / 0.7504) vs 0.7533; time 6.74 s vs 6.95 s (-3.0%; 272 vs 352 steps). -0.44 pp for -0.21 s ≈ 2.1 pp/s: far worse than the epoch line. Verdict: refutes H2.3.3 (fall 0.44 pp >= 0.3). Contingency 'opposite way': discard. Lesson: under Muon, the number of optimizer steps in the early phase matters (each Muon step is an orthogonalized, fixed-size move; halving the count while scaling lr by sqrt(2) under-trains), consistent with E017's finding that smaller batches (more steps) helped. The fixed per-step cost (~1.5 ms Muon) is not worth saving at the price of steps.
