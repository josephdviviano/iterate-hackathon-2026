## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted [0.7495, 0.7565]; got 0.7480. I trusted E029 ('3 vs 5 epochs of bias training neutral') and the CIFAR-10 speedrun practice (~0.25 epoch) to conclude the bias window barely matters. Root cause: the whitening bias acts as a learned per-channel threshold on the whitened patch features feeding the first GELU; with 100 classes and low-res early epochs its optimum moves for a few epochs, so freezing it at 8 steps leaves a poor threshold for the rest of training. The world model extrapolated a 'neutral between 3 and 5 epochs' finding down to 0.25 epoch. Lesson: neutrality over one range of a knob does not imply neutrality far outside it.
