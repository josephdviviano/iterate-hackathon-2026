Root cause: I assumed ~0.5-0.9 pp/epoch (diminishing returns), but the slope at 8.5->9.5 epochs is ~1.0 pp/epoch: this recipe is still deep in the under-trained regime on CIFAR-100, where every epoch matters a lot. Lesson: centre epoch-scaling predictions on ~1 pp/epoch in this regime and widen ranges by the per-mean noise (~0.15 pp).
