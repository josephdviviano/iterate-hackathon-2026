## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted accuracy [0.754, 0.762] and time [8.45, 8.90]; got 0.7514 and 8.901 (time just outside). Root cause (accuracy): I transferred E004's SGD finding (768 @ -1 epoch ≈ 576 accuracy, so 768 buys ~1 epoch) to Muon without evidence. Under Muon the width gain is at most ~0.2 pp, much less than 0.5 epoch (~0.5 pp), so the wider-and-shorter trade lost. The world model treated 'width in group 3 buys sample efficiency' as an optimizer-independent fact; it was established only for SGD. Plausible mechanism: Muon's orthogonalized updates make better use of the existing 576 channels (equalizing the update across singular directions), which is the same thing extra width gave SGD. Time: +7%/step for 768 (consistent with E004's +6%), plus the 576->768 shift happened at batch 2000 where the 576 baseline is already more efficient. Lesson: re-validate optimizer-dependent findings after an optimizer switch; under Muon, prefer epochs over width in group 3.
