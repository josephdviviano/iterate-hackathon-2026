# E058 · I064 · H1.8

H1.8 (inconclusive, c=0.35): On the resizing recipe, the marginal value of extra training is unresolved. Two single-salt runs showed no gain: E032 (+0.5 epoch at 32 px, -0.06 pp) and E036 (+1 epoch at 20 px, -0.27 pp). Both are confounded, because the warmup fraction, the Lookahead ramp and the whitening-bias window are defined over total steps and move when epochs are added. Each delta is about 1 SE. Whether the recipe is still under-trained (~0.5-1 pp/epoch) or near a plateau decides whether epochs can buy margin.
H1 (open, c=0.50): Margin, which is now the binding constraint, must come from time-free sample-efficiency knobs that were inherited from CIFAR-10 or SGD and never varied on this CIFAR-100 recipe: flip scheme, output regularization (head scale, label-smoothing form), the Muon-era SGD-group settings, Lookahead/schedule shape, and how schedule anchors sit relative to resolution phases. Gains of +0.1-0.2 pp would make the ~0.35 s of banked neutral cuts keepable.
Idea: Exploit, and measure the tail slope in the cheap direction now that margin exists. On the current tip, cut the 32 px phase from 4 to 3.5 epochs (16 fewer steps at batch 1536 with drop_last). Keep the LR triangle and Lookahead over total steps, as in E032, which added the same half-epoch. Change nothing else; no new graphs. Run the official seeds plus dev seeds 3-12. E032's +0.5 epoch at 32 px bought -0.06 pp on one draw, so the tail may be saturated and a trim could be cheap.
