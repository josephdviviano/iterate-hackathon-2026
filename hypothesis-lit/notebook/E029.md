# E029 · I036 · H2.4.4

H2.4.4 (open, c=0.40): Prediction: training the whitening-conv bias only in the first ~1-3 epochs and then stopping backward before the 32x32 whitening stage (a second CUDA graph captured in build) cuts ms/step by >=2% in the later epochs and total time by >=1.5%. The 3-seed mean stays within noise.
H2.4 (open, c=0.35): Per-step cost concentrates in the high-resolution stages (the 32x32 whitening output and the group-1 convs, plus their backward). The cause is low conv efficiency at small channel counts, not BN/activation memory bandwidth. Removing high-res work that carries little accuracy (first-layer backward, redundant passes) cuts time more cheaply than cutting epochs. Group-1 depth cannot go below 2 convs (E023).
H2 (open, c=0.72): Time is mainly set by per-step throughput: how fast each training step runs on the A100 (precision, memory layout, compilation, data pipeline, FLOPs per step), independent of how many steps are needed.
Idea: Exploit by removing high-resolution backward work that carries little accuracy. On E025, train the whitening-conv bias only during the 24 px phase (epochs 1-5). In the 28 px and 32 px phases, detach the whitening-layer output (after its activation) before group 1. Autograd then skips the dgrad of the first 128-channel conv and the whitening BN/activation backward at the highest resolutions. In the optimizer, drop the whitening bias from those phases' parameter list, or zero its LR. The 28 and 32 px graphs are captured in build with the detach already in place, and the 24 px graph is unchanged, so no new graph count and no recapture in the timed region. Log per-phase ms/step.
Expected (from the queue): 28/32 px ms/step falls 2-4%, so total time falls 1-2% (about -0.08 to -0.15 s, to about 7.2-7.27 s). This is work removed, so it should carry over to the PCIe judge (H5.1). Accuracy within ±0.15 pp of the pooled 0.7533, since airbench also freezes the whitening bias after about 3 epochs. Keep it if the 3-seed mean is >=0.753 and time is >=1% lower. If accuracy falls by >=0.25 pp, the whitening bias needs the full-res phase; refute H2.4.4.
