## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Time predicted [6.60, 6.70]; got 6.50 (better than predicted). I sized the saving from a one-layer intuition (a tiny pooling op). Root cause: adaptive_max_pool2d's backward is an atomic scatter kernel, not fused by inductor, and it runs at every step at all four resolutions on a [1536, 576, h, w] tensor; plus the adaptive op forced a graph boundary for fusion. The world model's profile lumped it into 'Triton BN/GELU/pool ≈ 5.6 ms' without isolating it. Lesson: non-fusable ops (atomic scatters, adaptive pools) can be worth ~0.5 ms/step even when tiny; look for them in the profiler's non-Triton kernels.
