## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Time predicted [7.8, 8.6]; got 7.49. I scaled per-step time with MACs (+19% MACs -> +15% time), but per-step time rose only 6%. Root cause: the 8x8 and 4x4 layers of group 3 are small GEMMs (N*H*W = 65k / 16k rows) that are not compute-bound at 576 channels; widening them raises arithmetic intensity, so extra channels there are nearly free. The world model wrongly treated time as proportional to FLOPs. Lesson: in this net, per-step time is not FLOP-proportional for late (low-resolution) layers; widening the deep layers is cheap, and the cost is concentrated in the high-resolution early layers (group 1 at 32x32 / 16x16). Predict width changes from measured ms/step, not MACs.
