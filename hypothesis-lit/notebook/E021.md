## Post-mortem

what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Predicted accuracy [0.7555, 0.7590]; got 0.7552. I assumed ~0.9 pp/epoch (E009->E014) and an E020 baseline of 0.7588. Root cause: E020's 0.7588 was itself partly a lucky seed-0 draw (0.7623 vs 0.757 for seeds 1-2); a schedule change re-rolls trajectories, so seed 0 regressed toward the mean. The world model treated the incumbent's 3-seed mean as the true expected accuracy; it has ~0.15-0.2 pp of trajectory noise. Lesson: when predicting a change from the incumbent, regress the incumbent toward its seed-median and widen ranges by ±0.2 pp; small epoch trims (<0.5 epoch) are not measurable against that noise.
