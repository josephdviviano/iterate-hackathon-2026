# E048 · I056 · H2.3.2

H2.3.2 (open, c=0.40): FixRes-style mechanism: low-res training costs little final accuracy because the closing 32 px phase only needs to re-fit BN running statistics and the head to the test resolution. With fast BN running-stat momentum (as in Fable and airbench94_muon), that phase can be shorter. Part of E020's gain over E015 may come from this, beyond seed luck.
H2.3 (supported, c=0.65): Progressive resizing (whole-image bilinear downsampling on a 20 -> 24 -> 28 -> 32 px ladder, with only the last ~4 epochs at 32 px) cuts step cost at little accuracy cost. Step cost concentrates in the high-resolution group-1 layers, so early epochs at low resolution are cheap, and the first epochs are insensitive to resolution down to 20 px.
H2 (supported, c=0.80): Time is mainly set by per-step throughput: how fast each training step runs on the A100 (precision, memory layout, compilation, data pipeline, FLOPs per step), independent of how many steps are needed.
Idea: Exploit by stacking two near-misses. On E038 (2x20+3x24+2x28+4x32 px, 2/3/3, Muon with AOL plus 2 NS steps, conv1 block frozen at 32 px, ls 0.4; 6.68 s), make two changes. (1) Re-apply E042's pooling cut: replace the head's AdaptiveMaxPool2d(1) with x.flatten(2).max(dim=2).values at every resolution and in eval. Use the indices-based backward, not amax, because amax crashed in E041. (2) Add E043's end-of-train BN re-estimation with 4 batches instead of 6. After the final Lookahead sync, reset all BN running stats. Re-estimate them with momentum=None from 4 forward-only batches of 1536 un-augmented 32 px training images, drawn with the trial-seeded generator, under no_grad, fp16 and channels_last. Then restore BN momentum. Put the re-estimation pass in a small graph or compiled function warmed up in build on synthetic data. Recapture all 4 train graphs in build. Check that eval gives finite [B,100] logits for B in {1, 7, 513, 1024}. Besides the official run, evaluate the same code on dev seeds 3-12 outside the official command, so the BN-re-estimation gain is measured against E046's 10-seed baseline of 0.7534 with SE about 0.09 pp, not against a single draw.
Expected (from the queue): Time about 6.52-6.58 s, down 1.5-2.4% from 6.68 s: -0.19 s from pooling and +0.04-0.05 s for 4 re-estimation batches. Official 3-seed accuracy 0.7525-0.757. Dev 10-seed mean 0.7535-0.7555, centred near +0.1 pp over 0.7534. Keep if official >=0.7535 and dev mean >=0.753. A dev gain >=0.1 pp supports the BN-mismatch part of H2.3.2 and makes this the margin base for the 28 px freeze and bias-window cuts. A dev gain <0.03 pp refutes it: then drop the re-estimation and re-apply the pooling cut alone with the next margin source.
