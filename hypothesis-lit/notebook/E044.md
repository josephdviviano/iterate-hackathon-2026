## Post-mortem
<!-- For failures and missed predictions: what you predicted, what happened, the root cause,
what the world model got wrong, the lesson. -->
Time predicted [6.85, 7.10]; got 7.38 (+10%). I reused E004's '+6% per step for 768' (SGD, batch 1024, no Muon). Root causes: (1) Muon's NS cost grows with the filter matrices (768x3456 and 768x6912 vs 576x...; the Gram matrices are 768x768), and the Muon update is a fixed per-step cost now that convs are cheaper; (2) the conv part of the step is now smaller (resizing, frozen block, detached whitening), so group 3 is a larger share of the remaining time than in E004; (3) +33% channels in group 3 also raise the 384->768 conv at 8x8/7x7 (the largest single conv by MACs). Lesson: re-measure per-step costs on the current recipe; old ms/step deltas don't transfer once the cost structure has shifted.
